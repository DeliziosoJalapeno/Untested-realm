2026-08-08

#### New / changed views ####
- The **Evil view** is now a full **Subtype view** (🏷 button, **Z** hotkey; **←/→** to cycle). A dropdown that peeks over the top of the board lets you pick any subtype actually present — **Spellcasters** (the default, includes avatars), **Evil**, Mortals, Undead, Beasts, Relics, Deserts, Rivers, Towers… — and every matching unit, site, artifact **and hand card** glows while the rest dims.

#### Table & layout ####
- **Bigger central board**: slimmer left action rail and top bar reclaim space for the play area.
- **Bigger left rail buttons** (matched to the new mana widget), with a little more breathing room between the rail and the board — taken from the right panel, so the board size is unchanged.
- New **mana widget** under your player bar (at hand level): shows **remaining / total** mana this turn (total = what you have left + what you've already spent).
- **Site ownership** is clear again — a bright blue (yours) / red (opponent) bar along the bottom of each site, drawn on top of the art so it can't be hidden.
- **Fewer minions render bigger**: a location with 1–2 minions shows them large, 3 medium, 4 as a 2×2, 5–6 smaller; 7+ still tile into the compact grid. Avatars included.
- **Auras** now sit centred on the shared corner of their four sites, so minions no longer fully cover the aura card.
- **Ground artifacts** moved to the mid-right of a site and render bigger on roomy sites; nearby minions shrink and shift so they never overlap the artifact, and multiple artifacts stack neatly on the border, clear of auras.
- **Tapped minions**: the stat line now reads **power / life**, with damage shown on the life number (e.g. `3/2(-1)`); tapped cards shrink just enough to never spill their numbers past the site border.
- Burrowed/submerged minions shown in the surface view moved to the right so they don't cover auras.
- The **Editor** panel is larger and can be dragged around by its header.
- The board column's up/down scrollbar no longer disappears when your hand holds only site cards.
- Clicking a unit shows a thicker, brighter highlight on the sites it can move to.

#### Fixes ####
- **Mobile layout no longer opens on PCs** — a touchscreen laptop (or a small desktop window) is no longer mistaken for a phone. Only genuine phones/tablets (no mouse/trackpad) get the mobile layout; `/mobile` or `?mobile` still forces the preview.
- **Boulevard of Bones** now summons its Skeleton when the **Geomancer** reshapes an adjacent Rubble into a real site — the reclaimed site is now truly "played" (its Genesis, ward, mana and on-play triggers all fire), like any other site.
- **Token minions carry their element** again — **Foot Soldiers are Earth, Skeletons Air, Frogs Water** — so **Crusade** ("allied earth minions +1"), **Jihad** and other element effects correctly count them. (The digital card data had dropped token elements.)
