2026-10-03

#### New ####

* **Replays.** Local games (pass-and-play and vs-computer) can now be saved and watched back. Hit **⬇ Save replay** on the game-over screen to download the game, then **▶ Watch a replay** on the home page to load it. The replay plays at a steady pace (1 move / 2s) with play/pause, 1×/2×/4× speed, step forward/back, a scrubber to jump anywhere, and a flip to watch from either player's side — with both hands revealed. Replay files are tiny (just the opening setup + the list of moves, replayed through the real engine).
* **Sound effects.** Cards now have sound: drawing, playing, tapping, untapping, shuffling, a chime at the start of each turn, a roll for random effects, and an alert on the red error message. Toggle it with the 🔊 / 🔇 button in the board buttons (remembered between sessions). Sounds courtesy of the open-source Cockatrice project.
* **A new look for the home page.** The landing page now showcases card art down both sides (desktop) — a wall of the game's trickiest cards — with a cursive signature under the title.

#### Card & Rules Fixes ####

* **Chaos Twister can blow a minion into the void.** If the blown minion lands on a void square (one with no site), it deals no damage — not even to itself — and is banished unless it has Voidwalk, exactly as the FAQ rules. Before, it could only ever land on a site or sail off the board.
* **The computer retreats the unit you attacked when it defends.** When the computer defends with another unit, it now pulls the originally-attacked unit out of the fight by default (the whole point of defending — saving it), keeping it in only when its blow is what finishes the attacker. It used to leave your target — even its own Avatar — in to take the hit.
