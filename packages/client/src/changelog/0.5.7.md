2026-10-31

#### Rules fixes ####
* **Storyline** (geleeroyale): events now resolve in the order the codex gives.
  * A trigger that asks something interrupts what set it off. A Snallygaster crossing a Troll Bridge now stops on the bridge until the toll is settled, instead of standing on the far side first.
  * Moves, defenders walking into a fight, and forced drags (Grapple Shot, Meat Hook, Pudge Butcher) go one step at a time. A unit killed or moved away meanwhile goes no further. Hotwheel and Bull Demons of Adum wait at a location whose trigger asked something, then strike there and roll on.
  * Simultaneous triggers: the non-active player's resolve first. The active player chooses the order of their own when two or more would act.
  * "When you cast" triggers always resolve before the spell (not only the Enchantress's).
  * A question from a card that has left the realm before it is answered is ignored.
* **Ward** stops "dies" effects. **The Black Plague** and **The Great Famine** now break a warded minion's ward instead of killing it. Since nothing died, the plague is then dispelled.
* **deathrite summons are no longer always on the surface** A token summoned "here" or "where it died" now appears in the dying unit's region. A Noisome Twosome buried by Cave-In dies underground, so its Skeleton is summoned underground, where it dies at once. It no longer appears on the surface. The same fix applies to Bitten, Novice Necromancer, Necromancer, Sir Morien, lingering Ghouls, Army of the Dead, Stygian Archers and Draconian Bonekite.

#### Card fixes ####
* **Hillock Basilisk** and **Stone-gaze Gorgons** now properly work with multi-step actions
* **Tabula Rasa** silences its bearer. Its "can't be modified" protection had made the bearer immune to Tabula Rasa's own silence, now it correctly exepmts it. 
* **Atlantean Fate / Flood**: their sites now dry up however the aura leaves play (destroyed, returned, banished), not only when Dispelled. A site flooded by something else stays flooded (geleeroyale).
* **Twist of Fate**: Death's Door can't be undone by swapping life. An Avatar at Death's Door stays at 0, while the other Avatar takes the 0 and arrives at Death's Door (Aadu).
* **Kairos the Archivist** no longer restores life totals (FAQ). This could pull an Avatar back from Death's Door.
* **Paying life** down to 0 (Demonic Contract, abilities with a life cost) now puts your Avatar at Death's Door. Demonic Contract no longer offers "pay 4 life" when you have less than 4 life.
* **Gift of the Raven** heals you on the *chosen* minion's next strike. Before, it fired on whichever ally struck first (Aadu).

#### Bot ####
* A computer **Pathfinder** now blazes its trail instead of wasting turns trying to play or draw sites (geleeroyale).
