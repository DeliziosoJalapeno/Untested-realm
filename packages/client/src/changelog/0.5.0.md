2026-10-15

#### Rules update (July & October 2026 codex) ####
* **Silence is now "printed only."** A silenced card loses only the abilities **printed on it** (and can't cast) — it **keeps** keywords and abilities granted by **other** sources. So a silenced minion still flies if an artifact grants it Airborne, still has Immobile from Entangle Terrain, and so on.
* **Mana is provided while you control the source.** If you **lose** a mana source mid-turn (a site destroyed, sacrificed, silenced, or taken), the mana it provided leaves your pool immediately — down to what you've already spent. (Gaining a source mid-turn already credited its mana.)
* **Stealth, Ward, and Lance are Genesis abilities** — granted as a one-time mark/token when the minion enters. A minion summoned while silenced never gains them; once gained, they're lost if it becomes silenced or disabled, exactly as the codex now specifies.

#### Card rulings (October 2026 FAQ) ####
* **FAQ view** now shows the **official verbatim text** for every card — all 400+ cards and 800+ rulings are pulled straight from the live codex, so the in-game FAQ matches the official wording exactly (no more paraphrases).
* **Vindictive Nation** now also makes an opponent **lose life for silencing it** (e.g. with **Smokestacks of Gnaak** or **Fields of Phyxis**) — the act of silencing it triggers its ability as it becomes silenced.
* **Saint of Redemption** now enters the realm **tapped** himself (as do minions arriving after him).
* **Sir Gawain** still **draws a card** when he dies shielding an ally, even if that death coincides with other damage simultaneously.
* **Lord of Fear** — a single unit **may** defend after all, as long as the originally attacked unit remains in the fight (it isn't defending *alone*).
* **Vindictive Nation** charges exactly **1 life per site** when **Atlantean Fate** floods several of its nearby allied sites.
* **Cloud City** can't fly onto **The Void**, and **Thin Ice** checks its "5+ power" when the ability resolves — both confirmed.
* **Key to the City** now fully implements **Protected from Sites** — its bearer ignores *all* site abilities, including on-entry hazards: it can be dragged onto a **Bottomless Pit** (or a flipped-Druid site, Dark Alley, etc.) and simply shrug it off.
* **Oversized units** (2×2 minions, Enchantress walls, Megamoeba-style blobs) are now **teleported legally** — the whole footprint must land on sites, on-board — and when you teleport one you get a **placement/rotation picker** (cycle the orientation; the footprint previews on the board) so you can face and fit it where you want.

#### Card and rules fixes (independent from rule updates)####
* **Ward** now correctly saves its bearer from being **destroyed** or **disabled**: a warded minion survives **Roots of Yggdrasil**'s collapse (Paladins of Bazia) and can't be cocooned/disabled by **Gargantula** — the ward breaks instead.
* **Midnight Rogue** (and any Stealth minion) now loses **Stealth** when it **blocks/defends**, not only when it attacks or moves.
* **Saint of Redemption**'s abilities ("no minion is Evil", "all enter tapped") correctly switch **off while he is disabled**.
* **Shrink** now works on **Animist Spirits** — it zeroes their base power (their mana-cost power) to 0/0, while leaving external buffs intact.
* **Detonate** can target a minion/artifact you **own even if an enemy controls it** (your infiltrated Siege Giant).
* A **stolen artifact** (e.g. a Toolbox taken from the opponent) is now usable — a carried artifact is controlled by its **bearer**, not its original conjurer.
* **Summoning Sphere** only opens summoning on the sites it actually **covers** (it was letting you summon minions anywhere).
* **Deathspeaker** echoing **Archangel Michael** (or any minion whose Genesis asks a question) now lets the full Genesis resolve before the echo fades — no more cut-short step/strike.
* **Dragonlord** taking a Dragon's aspect now also inherits its **passive text**, so **Caelestis**'s "next Dragon cast here is free" actually discounts the cast.
* An animated aura (**Wildfire** and friends) now **reverts to an enchantment at the start of your next turn even when its animator has gone** — if the Enchantress gets taken over by **Mephistopheles**, or a masked **Imposter** (or **Vivien the Enchantress**) did the animating, the minion no longer stayed a creature forever.
* Spells cast by an **Omphalos** (or a spellcaster **site**) now measure their range from the **caster itself**, not your avatar — e.g. **Major Explosion** can target up to two steps from the Omphalos, and its blast preview appears correctly.
* **Lord of the Void** is fixed all round: you can **skip** its end-of-turn "may banish" with **Esc**; a banished site properly becomes a **void** — everything on *or under* it (units **and** artifacts) falls in, a **Voidwalker** survives there while anything else is banished; and, since "adjacent" includes a card's own square, it may banish the **site it stands on** and ride into the void.
* **Kairos the Archivist**: **banished cards stay banished** (the archive can never bring them back), and nothing is left stranded on the surface of a square whose site wasn't restored.
* **Waveshaper**: a minion that was **already tapped** when the flood hit now still **misses its next untap** (it had been slipping free a turn early).
* **Quarrelsome Kobolds**' end-of-turn strike can no longer be aimed at a **Stealthed** enemy — it's a targeted strike.
* **Asmodeus** and **Frozen Horror** — their "**Break Stealth →**" abilities now actually fire (they were cancelling themselves out as the Stealth broke on activation).
* **Frontier Settlers**: the site they play now **fully runs its Genesis** (Tadpole Pool's frogs, a Pond's mana, etc.) instead of appearing inert, and the ability is correctly **one-shot** — gone once they settle.
* **Pact with the Devil** can now sacrifice a **spellcaster artifact** (an Omphalos) or a **spellcaster site**, not only a minion caster.
* **Raze** now destroys **carried** artifacts on the razed site too, not just loose ones.
* **Drown** now drags a submerged bearer's **carried artifact** under with it.
* **Disabled cards lose their passive/triggered abilities**, just like silenced ones. A **Hillock Basilisk** or **Stone-gaze Gorgons** disabled by a burrowed **Root Spider** no longer freezes anyone, a disabled **Lord of Fear** stops blocking lone defenders, and disabled movement-filters / step-granters / entry-triggers all switch off — and a silenced **Smokestacks of Gnaak** no longer silences its neighbours.

#### UI fixes ####
* The **cemetery, collection, and mulligan** popups are capped at **two rows** of cards (the rest scrolls), so they no longer break the game being uncloseable on certain screen ratios.
* **All popups stay on the board grid** now, as some were missed by previous fix of it (the attack-target prompt and others could spawn off-board or off-screen at some window sizes).

#### Quality-of-life ####
* The **Editor** is now usable while the **start-of-turn "draw a spell / draw a site"** prompt is up — the prompt tucks away so you can edit the board, and reappears when you close the Editor.
* **Ending your turn with an untapped avatar** now asks for confirmation first, so you don't skip playing a site by reflex.
* Removed the in-game **"Wait!"** button, as **undo** covers for it. Its main use was to draw a site with avatar in opponent's turn.
