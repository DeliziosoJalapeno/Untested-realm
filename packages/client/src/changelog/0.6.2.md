#### Frost Mage vs Lavamancer! ####
* **The whole set is in, with its official text and art.** The cards first added from the previews now have their official wording, and 35 cards are new (see the next section).
* **Fireball and Polar Bears are now the new versions.**
  * **Fireball** explodes in a plus shape: 4 to each unit where it hits and 2 to each orthogonally adjacent location. It no longer asks which unit it hit.
  * **Polar Bears** cost 3 and are 3/3.
  * The old Alpha/Beta art of both is an alternative art in the deck builder.
  * **Avatar of Fire**'s fire sites now act as the new Fireball.
* **Both precons are in Precon mode**, 101 cards each: the Avatar, 60 spells, 30 sites and a 10-card collection. The official list shows card names only, so the counts come from community lists that match it exactly.
* **Reprints take the current wording:** **Coy Nixie** taps the enemy "if it is untapped", **Hamlet's Ablaze!** and **Heat Ray** have their official text. **Scoria** and **Tundra** now read "the higher of the two".
* **Deep Snow** now says "one adjacent site" instead of "target", so it doesn't break a Ward.
* **The in-game FAQ is up to date**, with the 38 rulings published for the set and the other changes since October.

#### New cards ####
* **Ancient Thaw**: casts a Beast from your collection that is an **Alpha printing** (the "@" is the Alpha set symbol). Mark a card as Alpha by picking its "Alpha" art in the deck builder. A **Polar Bears** cast this way is the Alpha 2-cost 2/2, marked "Alpha printing" (FAQ). Precon copies are never Alpha, so in the Frost Mage precon it finds nothing.
* **Arctic Fox**: Stealth, can't be disabled. From your cemetery, pay (1) and banish it to give an ally "Stealth, Can't be disabled."
* **A Yeti So Shy**: Stealth, Outerbound. Under **Magellan Globe** no square is outer, so it is always disabled.
* **Bearserker**: with (W)(W), pay the difference in cost to transform a Mortal or Bear into Bearserker from your hand, untapped. Enemy minions count too, and stay under their controller. A "Bear" is any card with Bear in its name. It can't transform another Bearserker.
* **Chart** draws two sites; **Research** draws two spells.
* **Dust Devil**: Movement +1, takes no damage while attacking.
* **Flametongue Newt** (once on your turn) and **Sunspear Tribe** (once per game) throw a 1-damage projectile.
* **Frost Breath**: Beasts and Spirits can cast it. The units in its printed pattern take damage and are disabled until your next turn.
* **Full-tilt Goliaths**: Charge. Genesis silences the nearby sites until your next turn.
* **Gnarled Laborers** untap the first time they take damage each turn. A hit taken while they are disabled or silenced counts as that first time.
* **Hailstriker**: Ranged. Its Genesis targets an adjacent location: each minion there takes 1 damage and is disabled until your next turn.
* **Hibernal Legion**: at the end of your turn it freezes its site and the other minions there until it leaves the realm.
* **Highgate Guide**: nearby allies, the Guide included, are Protected from sites.
* **Humming Hummocks**: until your next turn, allies nearby the Hummocks are Protected from everything except units. The protection follows the site (FAQ), so an ally walking in gains it.
* **Jormungand**: occupies every outer location safely and can't be modified. It keeps that edge ring even under Magellan Globe, since it's a printed area.
* **Jussi Puffins**: Airborne, Submerge, and move as if the opposite edges of the realm were connected.
* **King Krakalak**: Genesis sacrifices its site to deal 5 to each other unit there and 3 at each of the eight sites around it.
* **Landslide** (trap): swaps itself with another adjacent site.
* **Man-made Ice Age**: minions and artifacts atop the affected sites are disabled.
* **Myrmidons**: Burrowing, +1 power for each site in their span of land.
* **Nanurluk**: has Charge while there are Bears or sites in your cemetery.
* **None Survive the Sun**: at the end of each turn, surface minions under it take 20 and the others 1. Then it moves one step west, from the first player's point of view (codex "East and West"), or is dispelled at the edge. Minions in the void don't burn, and under Magellan Globe it wraps around forever (FAQ).
* **Permafrost**: a disable on a minion here lasts until the minion leaves, then ends. Only a disable that actually landed is held, so a minion that can't be disabled, or a Yeti on an outer square, is never held.
* **Reed Cord Measurer**: Genesis plays or draws a site.
* **Runeling**: Spellcaster. Genesis draws a spell only he can cast.
* **Sissi Marksmen**: Ranged, and a shot north or south that misses may continue across the top or bottom edge. The aiming preview draws that wrapped lane.
* **Slag Slug**: whenever it moves off a site, it deals 2 damage to the units there. Dying or leaving the realm isn't "leaving" (codex).
* **Slide**: slides an ally or a disabled enemy as far as possible in one direction, then you draw a spell. It never slides into the void (FAQ). Under **Magellan Globe** it wraps across the joined edges to the farthest reachable location, and you choose if several tie; oversized minions too.
* **Stomping Mammoth**: while attacking, its excess damage goes to its own site. Each defender must be dealt lethal damage first, and power you leave unassigned counts as excess (FAQ).
* **Swealand Longship**: once on your turn, may move to an adjacent water site.
* **Terraform**: replaces the sites in its column with the top of each controller's atlas, from your back row forwards. A trap goes in face-down, and a site that can't be replaced stays (codex "Replace").
* **Tusslin' Trolls**: at the end of your turn, strike an adjacent minion for a D6. The target is chosen before the roll, and with nothing else adjacent they strike themselves (FAQ).
* **When the Peaks Fell**: snow rolls forward from the allied sites you choose and carries surface units as far as possible. Each carried unit takes 3 damage once and is disabled until your next turn. Snow that can't move affects no one (FAQ).

#### Official rulings and codex changes ####
* **Damage and disable at once.** **Frostbolt**, **Hailstriker**, **Frost Breath** and **When the Peaks Fell** deal their damage and disable together, so a minion they kill fires no Deathrite (FAQ).
* **Khamaseen Mummy** and **Fenvale Muse**: you control the opponent's site Genesis they trigger, so the threshold from a **Desert Bloom** goes to you (reversed FAQ).
* **Druid**: a silenced site keeps the flip-side ability; Bedrock still doesn't get it (reversed FAQ).
* **Atlantean Fate** counts as silence: **Vindictive Nation** in its area triggers
* **Can't be modified**: such a unit can't have a Ward or Stealth mark (codex). **Monks of Kobalsa** can still be warded ("except by Wards").
* **Bedrock can't be modified, so nothing floods, silences, disables or transforms it.** Under **Atlantean Fate** it keeps its own threshold instead of only providing water, and **The Flood**, **Drought**, **Blightstone**, **Hibernal Legion**'s freeze, **Consecrate** and **Desecrate** leave it alone.
* **Protection works everywhere.** A Protected unit (**Key to the City**, **Highgate Guide**, **Humming Hummocks**) can't be targeted, damaged, destroyed, banished, modified, moved or impeded by the sources it's protected from. That covers walls and Blizzards blocking its attacks or movement, and spells that ask you to pick a unit.
* **No artifact bonuses while protected from artifacts**, even from its own carried artifacts.
* **Not protection:** **Failed Mutation**, **Amulet of Niniane**, **Arcade of Bones** and **Thing in the Ice** only stop targeting and damage from magic, so an untargeted spell can still disable them.
* **A transformed minion keeps its damage** (codex Transform): **Sir Gareth**, **Slimy Mutants**, **Shapeshift**, **Monstermorphosis**, **Sinister Pond** and **Gilman House** no longer heal it.
* **Genesis targets may be skipped**, Hailstriker's included.

#### Rules fixes ####
* **Targeted sites and locations must be in the caster's region.** Only cards that say "target" are affected; distance limits are unchanged.
  * A surface caster can target any site, and any location with a site. It can no longer throw a **Lightning Bolt** into the void, underground or underwater.
  * A void caster can target only void locations, and no sites.
  * An underground caster can target only land sites and the underground beneath them. An underwater caster can target only water sites and the water beneath them.
  * A site casting a spell counts as both its surface and its own subsurface.
* **Lance on effect strikes.** A strike made by an effect (Whirling Blades, Falling Star, any card that says "strikes") gets the Lance's +1 against units and breaks it, like a fight or Ranged strike.
* **Silence.** A silenced unit can't cast spells, even one it was granted. A minion that enters already silenced (Sisters of Silence, Bower of Bliss) gets no Stealth, Ward or Lance.
* **Stealth on a missed shot.** A Ranged shot or **Colicky Dragonettes**' fireball that hits nothing keeps the shooter's Stealth, because it dealt no damage. Firing still counts as interacting, so the shooter can't Drop this turn.
* **A silenced silencer silences nothing.** **Fields of Phyxis** silenced by Sinterfee, Smokestacks of Gnaak or Acid Rain no longer silences the site in front of it.
* **A sacrifice ignores Ward**, so **Vesuvius** and **Sinkhole** sacrifice a warded site too.
* **Minions covering several squares move whole.** A wall or a minion stretched over several squares that was pushed or teleported (Windblast, Grapple Shot, harpoons, Springstem Meadow…) used to leave its other parts behind.

#### Card fixes ####
* **Archimago** echoing a spell while carrying **Ring of Morrigan**: the echoed spell no longer vanishes while the Ring asks whom to bleed. Before, the echoed Lightning Bolt dealt no damage.
* **Mudslide**: the slide still spans the void, but a unit only slides onto another site. With the void beside it, the unit stays put, because a forced step can't push into the void.
* **Rat King**: every chosen rat is summoned, and you pick the adjacent site for each one.
* **Colicky Dragonettes**: no direction question when every direction hits the same thing.
* **Balor of the Evil Eye** also gazes at the other location of his own square: beneath his site, or the surface above him when he's subsurface.
* **Earl of the Ivory Towers** is a Spellcaster for every spell except Ordinary ones.
* **Hollow One** wakes up when a Mortal or Beast dies nearby; its disable no longer stops it from noticing.
* **Sir Perceval**'s quest follows its site when the site moves (Earthquake…). Two Percevals keep separate quests, and finishing one starts a new quest.
* **Vengeful Heir** enters as the copied Knight: it gets that Knight's Lance, Stealth or Ward, and its Genesis (a copied Sir Perceval starts his quest).
* **Undertow**: a Submerge minion can dive or surface in place as its step, and you choose surface or underwater when it moves to another square.
* **Thin Ice** ignores submerged minions when it counts the power on top.

#### Computer player ####
* The Thinking computer looks at moves faster on busy boards, especially with area-damage spells.
* An artifact a unit can carry is cast into a unit's hands rather than onto a bare site.
* Minions with a targeted Genesis are cast with their Genesis targets.
* **Cornerstone** is played to an open corner.
* Oversized (2x2) units can attack enemies and sites on every square they stand on.
* **Abundance** and other mana auras are cast over the computer's own sites, not the opponent's.
* The Fast computer aims an echoed Lightning Bolt at enemies, not at its own empty site.

#### Interface ####
* **Damage numbers on minions.** A Ranged shot, a single projectile or a targeted spell or ability now floats its damage over the minion it hit, even when the hit kills it. Area damage (Rain of Arrows, Balor's gaze, volleys) shows as big numbers on the sites instead.
* **Life gained shows on the Avatar.** A green +N rises above an Avatar when its player gains life (only what was actually gained, after the life limit).
* **Spell and ability arrows.** When anyone casts a spell or uses an ability, arrows run from the caster to each square it affected. The card panel shows the card, who played it, and what happened.
* **Sir Perceval's quest flag.** A 🚩 marks each Perceval's quest site and lights up while you hover or select that Perceval.
* **Only legal squares are clickable** when you pick a target square: a square out of range or outside the caster's region does nothing.
* **Search the game log.** Type in the box above the log to filter it; the log now keeps the last 500 lines.
* **Larger site cards** in the in-game detail panel.
* **Lateral buttons are shorter** in the in-game detail panel.
* **Meta decks.** The deck picker now lists one tournament deck for each of the ten most-played archetypes.
* **Encased in ice.** A minion encased by the **Frost Mage** looks frozen, its card tinted blue and cracked like ice, until the ice melts.
* **Alpha printings in the art picker.** Beasts list their Alpha printing separately from Beta, for **Ancient Thaw**. Many Frost Mage vs Lavamancer reprints also have their new art as an alternative.
* A minion that became another card (Vengeful Heir as the copied Knight) shows the new card's art.
