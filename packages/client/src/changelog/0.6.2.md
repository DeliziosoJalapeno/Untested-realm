2026-11-14
#### Frost Mage vs Lavamancer is out ####
* **All 120 cards of the set are in**, with their released text and official art: the 35 cards that had not been previewed are new, and the 34 previewed ones now use the printed wording.
* **Fireball** and **Polar Bears** are a single card each again, with the new printing's rules (Fireball's cross-shaped blast; Polar Bears cost 3 with 3 power). The separate "Fireball 2" and "Polar Bears 2" are gone; decks that used them keep working.
* **Precon mode**: **Frost Mage** and **Lavamancer** are now the full printed deck lists (60 spells and a 10-card collection each; 34 sites for Frost Mage, 30 for Lavamancer) and replace the "(previews)" lists.
* Both decks are also in the deck picker as **Frost Mage (FvL precon)** and **Lavamancer (FvL precon)**, so you can edit them or play them against any other deck.
* The reprinted cards offer their Frost Mage vs Lavamancer printing in the art picker.

#### New rules ####
* **Outerbound** (A Yeti So Shy): disabled unless it stands on the outside of the realm.
* **Protected from sites** (Highgate Guide) and **Protected from all but units** (Humming Hummocks): site abilities and magic can't target, damage, move, disable or stop the protected unit.
* **Permafrost**: a disable on a minion there doesn't run out, and lifts when it leaves.
* **Hibernal Legion** freezes its site and the other minions there until it leaves the realm.
* **Jormungand** occupies every outer location and can't be modified.
* **Jussi Puffins** move across the edges of the realm; **Sissi Marksmen** shoot across the top and bottom edges.
* **Stomping Mammoth**'s excess attack damage goes to the site it stands at, your own included.
* **Ancient Thaw** casts an Alpha Beast from your collection; Polar Bears cast this way are the Alpha 2-cost 2-power printing (official FAQ).
* **None Survive the Sun** burns the surface at the end of each turn and moves one step west until it sets.
