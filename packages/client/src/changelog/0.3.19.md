2026-08-09

#### UI Improvements ####
- **Animation on spell cast** , highlighting who cast the spell and where it did hit
- **damage grid visualization** for cards like craterize and lava flow
- **Battle panel** — after a battle both players get a recap. Shows for 5s, click / Skip to dismiss
- The **damage-splitting** panel now shows current life of enemy minions in the fight
- Earth's colour is now a **brownish-yellow**, not green.
- **Board coordinates** appear when a prompt refers to them, and multi-route moves draw a glowing **arrow along every candidate path**.
- The unit / site / artifact action buttons now pop out of the **minion itself**, and the move/attack chooser appears **under the destination**.

#### General fixes ####

- **"Find a battle" is reliable** — guests whose name clashes with a registered account queue as Guest instead of being blocked, and dead connections are dropped by a heartbeat, so you're never paired with a ghost or left searching forever.
- **big movement +n overhaul* on a multi-step move — the departed squares matter (Root Spider, Blaze, Giant Shark). The route picker is drawn under the destination and can be dragged aside, and it now appears when you **move-and-attack** too, not just for plain moves. Very large or region-crossing moves ask **Auto** (take a shortest route) or **Manual** — in Manual you **walk the unit one step at a time** (up to its full movement), only the legal next steps light up, and you choose the order — e.g. **burrow-then-move vs move-then-burrow** to reach an adjacent site's underground. Free steps don't cost you a step, and if you stop short you're asked to confirm or cancel (cancel does nothing at all).

#### Editor fixes ####
- **Give control** of a minion to the other player.
- **Cast an aura** onto the board and **cast a magic** straight from the Editor.
- Switching a card's type now fills in a **sensible default card** instead of always Foot Soldiers.

#### Computer opponent ####
- The **computer opponent** plays more aggressively and more sensibly: smarter about burrowing / submerging, diving into the void near your avatar, and only casting a magic when it actually helps.


#### Card Fixes ####

- **King of the Realm / Returned King** — minions they seize are handed **back** to their owner when the King dies or leaves.
- **Skirmishers of Mu** no longer loose their volley when first cast (only when they move).
