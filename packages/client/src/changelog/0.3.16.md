2026-08-07

#### Online ####
- Rejoin your own games from anywhere while signed in. Your seat is now remembered by your account (not just a per-browser token), and a “🎮 Your games” list on the home page lets you hop back into any room you’re seated in — public or private — from any browser or device. (If you’re logged in as krobbi and there’s a krobbi-vs-jalapeno room, you can rejoin as krobbi from anywhere.)
- Sealed: disconnecting no longer loses your opened cards — rejoining restores your pool and in-progress deck (the server keeps them tied to your seat). Fixed a follow-up where reloading the page mid-deckbuild still emptied your pool down to the supplied cards: your booster-opening progress is now remembered per room, and coming back with a deck already started re-reveals your whole pool.

#### UI fixes ####
- Fixed the board grid getting horizontally squished at some window sizes (a side-effect of the new desktop scaling): auras, 2×2 minions and their placement markers line up with the site grid again — the squares no longer shrink to fit, so the whole board scales uniformly.
- Site squares now use the true landscape shape of the site art, so site cards are shown in full with no cropping.
- Desktop table redesign: the game fills the whole window (no letterbox bars), with a bigger central board. Action buttons (Subsurface, Editor, Undo, Concede, End turn) moved to a square-icon rail on the left — with a new **Symbols** button (and **Q** hotkey) that opens a legend explaining every on-board symbol (Airborne, Ward, Stealth, summoning sickness, carried, masked…). The top bar now lists the keyboard shortcuts. Removed the redundant life/mana panel above your hand (it duplicated your player bar). New hotkeys: **E** editor, **Backspace** undo, **Q** symbols.
- The **Symbols** legend is on mobile too — a ❔ button in the left action menu opens the same explanation panel.

#### Card fixes ####
- Torshammar Trinket: a minion kept alive by its +1 power (a minion’s power is also its life, so the Trinket raises how much damage it can take) now survives to the next turn. The Trinket returns to hand only after end-of-turn damage is cleared, so e.g. a 1/1 with 1 damage carrying it no longer dies the instant the turn ends. Damage-dealing end-of-turn effects (like Wildfire) still kill as before. (The same holds for single-turn power-ups like Overpower, Gift of the Wolf and Gigantism.)
- Imposter: the mask now cracks off on ANY damage to the Imposter — spells (Heat Ray), abilities and projectiles (Redbreast Robin), and strikes — not just attacks. Previously damage to the avatar skipped the “when damaged” check entirely.
- Deathspeaker: you now choose where the flickered copy of a dead minion appears, so its Genesis (and any “when a creature enters nearby” triggers) resolve at the spot you pick, instead of always at your avatar’s square.
- Ward now correctly prevents ALL of a simultaneous strike. When a warded minion attacks (or is attacked) and is struck by two or more units at once — e.g. multiple defenders — the single Ward absorbs the whole simultaneous blow, as the rules require. Previously it stopped only the first strike and let the rest through. (Single strikes already worked; combat, spell and ability damage are all prevented by Ward.)
