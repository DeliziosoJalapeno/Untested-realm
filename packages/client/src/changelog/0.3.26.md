#### Direction picker — new ####
- **Directional effects now show arrows** When you pick a direction (Snowball, Fireball, Firebolts, Heat Ray, Ice Lance, Ball Lightning, Magic Missiles, Ice Shards, every Ranged unit's Shoot, and *any* "which direction?" card), glowing arrows flow from the source out to the **farthest square the effect actually reaches** — stopping at voids and projectile-blocking sites. Four arrows mark the origin; **hovering a direction lights up its lane and dims the others**; arrows are **coloured by the card's element**; and the picker panel spawns off to the side so it never covers the arrows.

#### Card & rules fixes ####
- **Quarrelsome Kobolds** now can strike an enemy sharing their **own** site, not only orthogonally-adjacent units.
- **Snowball** — units it can't push into a site (e.g. blocked by an enemy **Bailey**) are **dropped before that site** and no longer count for the damage. Also snowball now **stops before Impenetrable Copse** (or any site that blocks projectiles), leaving units sheltered there untouched.
- **Lethal now applies to fights** — **Blaze of Glory**'s fighter kills with Lethal, including a *temporary* one (**Gift of the Serpent**).
- **Darkest Dungeon** no longer activates when the Avatar can't be moved out of its area (**Sphere of Animosity**) and the Dungeon lies outside it — the trap stays armed for a later legal strike.
- **Monuments** can no longer be picked up — they're immovable.
- **Pathfinder**: trail-blazing prompt no longer spoils the name of your face-down topmost site.
