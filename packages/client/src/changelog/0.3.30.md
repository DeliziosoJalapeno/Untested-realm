2026-08-24

#### Editor ####
- **The Editor is now a multi-page menu.** The old wall of buttons is split into clear tabs — **Table** (life, mana, thresholds, draw, untap), **Create** (materialize any card to hand / cemetery / collection / board), **Modify**, and **Avatars** — so you can find a tool without hunting.
- **Select-to-modify for everything.** Sites, artifacts and auras now get the same per-object modify panel that minions have: pick one from its list, or click it on the board with the Editor open, and its own tools appear. Clicking an aura targets its **card image** (not its whole footprint), so the site underneath and any overlapping/stacked auras stay reachable. Auras can now be sent to the cemetery or removed from the game outright — previously they had no Editor controls at all.

#### Card fixes ####
- **Magician mulligan** no longer costs you a card. Mulliganing a site was returning it to the (nonexistent) atlas and redrawing nothing, so you started with six cards instead of seven — the Magician keeps its sites in the spellbook, so they now return there and are redrawn correctly. Spellslinger (7) and Duplicator (4) opening hands verified to survive a mulligan too.
- **Bone Jumble & the bone-raisers** (also **Barrow Wight**, **Fowl Bones**, **Zombie Bruiser**). Their *"(N), sacrifice a Skeleton token → summon this from your cemetery there"* ability now lives on the card, so you can activate it straight from the grave (pick which Skeleton to sacrifice) as well as by clicking a Skeleton token — you're no longer forced to go through the Skeleton.
- **Vivien the Enchantress** copies it, wherever she is. With a spellcaster bone-raiser in the realm, a dead Vivien can be raised by sacrificing a Skeleton — her "summon this" self-reference correctly resolves to *her* card. The Skeleton token also offers to raise such a Vivien.
