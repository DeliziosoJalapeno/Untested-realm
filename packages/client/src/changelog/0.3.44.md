#### Card & Rules Fixes ####

* **Toolbox & Silver Bullet send the spell to your graveyard.** When you fetch and cast a spell from your collection, the card now goes to your cemetery once it resolves — like any other cast — instead of quietly vanishing.
* **Legion of Gall actually banishes.** Cards it banishes from a collection are now truly gone from that collection's fetch pool: Silver Bullet, Toolbox, the Malleus and friends can no longer pull a card the Legion carried off.
* **You pick which way an enemy is pushed.** When the Screamer shoves an adjacent enemy away (or the Unland Angler reels one in) and more than one direction is legal, *you* — the effect's controller — choose it, as the rules intend. It was quietly letting the other side decide, so against the computer you never got the choice.
* **A hook/grapple blocked short no longer fights from nowhere.** If Pudge Butcher's or Meat Hook's drag — or a Grapple Shot — is stopped before it arrives (a Perilous Bridge, Cage of Sidrak…), there's no fight/strike and no phantom "Fight?" prompt. Grapple Shot in particular would otherwise strike the target from afar.
* **Poisonous Dagger makes the Ring of Morrigan's bleed lethal.** A carried artifact's damage now counts as the bearer's, so the bearer's Lethal (and kill credit) applies — the Ring's 1 damage finishes any minion while you hold the Dagger.
* **The Rack works on every strike, and on every part of a stretched victim.** It now triggers on effect strikes too (e.g. a Grapple Shot arrival), and striking an already-stretched Avatar on any of its squares racks it further — there's no privileged "main body" square.
* **Defending an oversized unit uses the real fight location.** When an oversized (or Rack-stretched) unit is attacked, the fight happens where the attacker engaged it, and eligible defenders are judged by their distance from *that* square — not the unit's far anchor.
