#### Card & Rules Fixes ####

* **Toolbox & Silver Bullet send the spell to your graveyard.** When you fetch and cast a spell from your collection, the card now goes to your cemetery once it resolves — like any other cast — instead of quietly vanishing.
* **Legion of Gall actually banishes.** Cards it banishes from a collection are now truly gone from that collection's fetch pool: Silver Bullet, Toolbox, the Malleus and friends can no longer pull a card the Legion carried off.
* **You pick which way an enemy is pushed.** When the Screamer shoves an adjacent enemy away (or the Unland Angler reels one in) and more than one direction is legal, *you* — the effect's controller — choose it, as the rules intend. It was quietly letting the other side decide, so against the computer you never got the choice.
