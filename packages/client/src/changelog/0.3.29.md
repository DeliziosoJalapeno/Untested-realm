#### Interface ####
- **Picking up & dropping artifacts, your choice.** When a unit shares a square with two or more artifacts it could carry, picking up now opens a card menu so you choose exactly which ones — no more grabbing the whole pile. Dropping works the same way: if a unit could set down more than one artifact, a menu lets you pick which to release. A lone artifact (or a lone droppable one) is still one click.
- **Every target glows.** Any legal target — unit, site, or artifact (on the ground *or* carried) — now carries the same soft gold highlight and brightens on hover, so it's always clear what you can click. Previously only minions lit up, leaving targetable sites and artifacts looking inert.
- **Lethal 🗡 and Ranged 🏹 badges** sit beside a unit's attack/defence, so you can read those keywords at a glance without opening the card. **Lethal** also joins the Subtype view's keyword filters.
- **Mulligan status.** While you decide your opening hand, the mulligan panel now tells you whether your opponent is still choosing or already locked in and waiting on you.
- **Sprawling bodies are drawn in full.** A creature that occupies more than one location — a **Megamoeba** or **Aethermoeba** trailing pseudopods, an Avatar stretched on **The Rack** - now shows ghostly copies of itself at every occupied site.
- **Yog-Sothoth** fills the realm with its terrifying presence (try it!)
- **Editor: movement ±1.** The Editor's unit panel gains a *move +1 / −1* control alongside power, to grant or strip a unit's extra steps (a unit never drops below 0 steps).
- **Editor: genesis and no genesis summon** Default summon now triggers genesis of the summoned minion. Old summon is kept as no genesis summon. This might be a little buggy for some cards (e.g. cards don't die in the void) but that's a feature, not a bug, as you might want to force the summoning of a minion in otherwise deemed impossible places. 
- **Burrow / Submerge / Surface button** simplified button for vertical movement, old system still in place.
- **Buried & submerged cards sit tidily when tapped.** no more out of bonds cards.

#### Card fixes ####
- **Bury & Drown** can now target artifacts, not just minions. Burying a *carried* artifact drags its bearer underground with it (an artifact-laden minion needs Burrowing to survive down there, or it's buried alive); a *submerged* one pulls the bearer under the waves the same way. An Avatar bearer can't go below, so only the artifact is buried/drowned — it slips from the Avatar's grip and sinks alone.
- **Arjaro Exorcist** can now correctly banish a nearby **aura**
- **Deathspeaker** bug solved that made it fail on some target needing genesis effects (e.g gargantula)
- **Vindictive Nation** now punishes an enemy who shatters a nearby allied **City of Glass**
- **Yog-Sototh** no longer randomly airborne 
- **Blaze of Glory** now fights each nearby enemy **one at a time**, in an order you choose
- **Imperial Road** fixed, now opponent can always play its site when conditions are met
- **Imposter** now works when masked as **Interrogator**
- **Wildfire** fixed as an animated aura, now taking 3 damage at the end of turn per its own effect
- **Route picker & Updraft Ridge.** Picking a movement path no longer silently auto-resolves when a **free step** is involved (an Airborne minion gliding off Updraft Ridge). Routes are measured by their step *cost*, not raw length, so a square reachable only by spending the free glide is offered like any other — whether the ridge is your starting square or merely lies along the way.
- **Magellan Globe** fixed again, with 100+ interaction bugs solved systemically.
