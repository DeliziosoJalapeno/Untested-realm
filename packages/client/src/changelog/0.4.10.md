2026-10-12

#### Report a bug ####
* **New: report a bug mid-game.** A 🐛 **Report bug** button now sits in the in-game toolbar (desktop rail and mobile menu). Describe what went wrong and it automatically attaches the **replay up to that point** plus a **full snapshot of the board**, so it can be reproduced exactly. Works in every mode; signed-out guests can report too (they just don't get a reply).
* **Reply notifications.** When an admin replies, the reporter sees a dismissable notice about their bug on the Home screen.

#### Rules & card fixes ####
* **Moon Clan Werewolf** — its "Sacrifice an allied Mortal → Summon from your hand" is now offered **on each of your Mortals** (click a Mortal to sacrifice it and have the Werewolf burst out on its square), exactly like a Skeleton raising a bone-raiser — instead of hiding on your avatar. (It's still a normal 3-cost summon too.)
* **Pith Imp** — a filched spell is now genuinely **held by the Imp**: only the Imp may cast it, and only while it's a **Spellcaster** (so a spellcaster-granting effect lets it unleash the stolen spell). If the Imp leaves the realm with the spell unspent, it **returns to its owner's hand**.
* **Maelström** — pulling a minion that's **carrying** other units (or a minion **swallowed by a Brobdingnag Bullfrog**) now drags the cargo along with its carrier instead of yanking it out on its own; a swallowed minion is never targeted by the pull.
* **Earthquake** — can no longer **move a void**. The rearrangement shuffles **sites** among the squares that hold them; empty squares stay put (a site can't slide onto one).
