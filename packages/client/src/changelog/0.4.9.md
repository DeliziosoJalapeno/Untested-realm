2026-10-11

#### Ward & Evil ####
* **Evil minions can't be warded** — now properly enforced. You can't place a Ward on an Evil minion (printed Evil **or** one a Corruptor has made Evil), and a warded minion that *becomes* Evil — e.g. your **Corruptor** + **King of the Realm** claiming an enemy's warded Mortal — **immediately loses its Ward**.
* **Persecutor** — branding an enemy "as Evil" is no longer treated as *real* Evil: it no longer turns them into a **Monster** and no longer **breaks their Ward**. It only makes your cards treat them as a valid Evil target this turn.
* **Accursed Albatross** — its death-curse now **respects Ward**: a warded ally caught by the curse has its Ward broken instead of being destroyed.
* **Revered Revenant** — now counts an allied Ward on its **own square** (a co-located warded ally, or the warded site it stands on), not just the four adjacent squares — matching the rulebook's "adjacent includes your own square."

#### Rules & card fixes ####
* **Archimago** — now asks **which spellcaster** casts the echoed spell whenever you control more than one, for *any* spell (not just projectiles — who casts can trigger "whenever you cast" effects, Sturmgeist, element locks, etc.). The echoed copy also **vanishes** when it resolves instead of returning to your cemetery, so you can't re-echo the same copy in a loop.
* **Merlin's Tower** — its "costs ③ less" now applies to **Magic only**; minions, artifacts and auras cast through the tower pay full price.
* **Artifacts in the void** now rise to the surface when a site is played **under** them (just like units), instead of being stranded in the void.
* **Hotwheel** rolling to a stop on a damaging site (a flipped **Druid**'s thorns, etc.) no longer takes an **extra hit** on its final square — a just-summoned unit whose Genesis moves it was entering its last square twice.

#### Quality-of-life ####
* **Persecutor's ability is fully backable** — a "↩ Go back" is offered through the whole "step toward Evil / brand an enemy" flow, and your once-per-turn use is spent only when a step or brand actually happens, so a misclick costs you nothing.
