2026-10-08

#### GUI fixes ####
* **Mobile** — the "waiting for an opponent" room screen now scrolls, so the Leave button is always reachable.

#### Rules & card fixes ####
* **Bureau of Occult Control** now works with **Deathspeaker**, **Archimago**, **Toolbox**, **Silver Bullet**, **Harvest Festival**, **Estranged Loner**, and **Young Master Damion**.
* **Deathspeaker** — you can back out of the "which minion / where" prompts freely now; your turn's use is spent only once the echo actually summons.
* **Earthquake** — the rearrange panel has a "↩ Go back" so you can cancel the cast until you confirm the new layout.
* **Wave of Eviction** now sweeps enemy **avatars** too, not just their minions.
* **Cave-In / Earthquake / Stormy Seas** now burrow/submerge an artifact **carried by your avatar** (the avatar stays up top, its gear goes under).
* **Order of the Sacred Oak** only stops **destruction**: you can still move, modify, and willingly sacrifice protected sites — and its protection switches off while it's silenced or disabled.
* **Fourberie Knot** — its bearer now really *is* a Faerie, so Faerie-matters effects (A Midsummer Night's Dream, Seelie/Unseelie Court, Fae City, The Faerie Queene…) count it.
* **Range-limited spells & abilities** — when a card reaches "up to two/three steps away," only locations actually in range are clickable now, so you can't aim too far and waste the card. Covers **Minor / Major Explosion**, **Mortality**, **Exorcism**, **Unravel**, **Dispel**, **Disenchant**, **Boil**, **Extinguish**, **Stone Rain**, **Incinerate** (near you or an allied Dragon), **Sleep**, **Siege Ballista**, and the **Midland Army** / **Corpse Catapult** bombardments. An oversized caster (e.g. one stretched by the Rack) can reach anything within range of *any* part of its body.
* **"Steps" now route around the void** — distance is counted in real steps between locations, not straight-line, so a target with a void between it and the caster is correctly farther away (or unreachable), never "2 steps away." Fixes every "up to N steps away" card above.
* **Meteor Shower** — as you choose the first, then second impact site, the board narrows to only the sites that share no border with your earlier picks; a bordering site simply can't be selected. The final confirmation grid then previews all three blasts before you commit.
* **Water sites judged consistently** — **Riptide**, **Marine Voyage**, **Mariner's Curse**, **Ormund Harpooneers**, **Sir Pelleas**, **Stinging Kelp**, **Waveshaper**, **Wave of Eviction**, and **Wrath of the Sea** now all decide "is this a water site?" the same way (honouring flooding, drought, and effect-granted water), matching Boil / Baptize / Stormy Seas.
* **Rubble can be flooded** — **Flood**, **Great Old One**, and **Wrath of the Sea** now flood rubble (flooded rubble becomes a body of water), instead of skipping it. Wrath of the Sea also submerges an artifact carried by your avatar.
* **Rubble is an Ordinary site** — a destroyed site no longer counts as its old Elite/Unique self: **Castle Haunt**, **Castle's Ablaze!**, and **Heirloom Lost** can't treat a pile of rubble as a grand castle anymore (and **Hamlet's Ablaze** may sit on it).
