2026-10-16

#### Card rulings & bug fixes ####
* **"Adjacent" now includes the same site, everywhere.** By the rulebook a location is adjacent to *its own square*, so a unit sharing your site is adjacent to you. Fourteen cards wrongly ignored the same site and only looked at the four neighbours — now fixed: **Shatter Strike** (an ally can finally hit an enemy standing on its own site), **Sir Gawain / Sir Agravaine / Sir Yvain**, **Sturmgeist**, **Shackled Demon**, **Profane Revenant**, **Ignis Rex**, **Megalurker**, **Ormund Harpooneers**, **Talamh Dréig**, **Unland Angler**, **Riptide**, and **Bodach Bogeyman**.
* **Drowning is now a general rule.** Whenever a minion *without* **Submerge** ends up underwater and dies for it — pushed under by **Drown**, caught by a flooding site, forcibly relocated, or stripped of Submerge by disable/silence — it counts as drowning. So **Watery Grave** raises a Skeleton for *any* enemy that drowns nearby, not only one submerged by a particular effect.
* **Whirling Blades** steps are real steps. A **Voidwalk** ally in the void can whirl up onto the surface (striking whatever it passes), an **Airborne** ally steps diagonally, and **Burrowing / Submerge** allies change level — instead of being locked into their starting region.

#### Online / interface ####
* **Declare a defender's route.** Sending a far-off unit to defend now walks it in along a route you choose — arrows or manual, just like a normal move — and only asks for the route when there's more than one. A defender that reaches the fight but is **disabled on arrival** (a Faerie stepping onto a Cold Iron Rod) doesn't count, and the attacked unit is offered a stay/withdraw choice only when a defender *actually* arrived.

#### More fixes — thanks, geleeroyale ####
* **geleeroyale** contributed eight further rules/bot fixes (found while running the engine on [realms.cards](https://realms.cards)): the forced **first site** always lands under your avatar (**Imperial Road** no longer strands an opponent's domain); **Rift Valley / Mirror Realm** can be played where another card dictates, and **Rift Valley** can pull apart a lone site; **Sea Raider** plunders the *killed enemy's* spellbook; **The Manor at Daperyll Hill** and **Slimy Mutants / Sir Gareth** are only offered while they have a legal target; **Waveshaper** keeps a permanently-flooded site (Atlantean Fate) flooded when its wave moves on; and the computer no longer summons a minion onto a square where it arrives disabled.
