2026-08-08

#### New views ####
- New **FAQ view** (📖 button, **F** hotkey; also on mobile): the board dims and every card that has an official ruling glows with a golden border — hover it on desktop, or tap it on mobile, to read its FAQ in the side panel. Card action panels are disabled while the view is on, so you can browse rulings safely mid-game. Covers 369 cards / 739 rulings, parsed straight from the official FAQ.
- New **Evil view** (**Z** hotkey; also on mobile): every Evil creature (Demon/Undead/Monster or branded — including things a Corruptor has corrupted) glows red, and everything else is dimmed, so you can see the board’s alignment at a glance. The Evil button/badge use a red pentagram (white on the button).

#### UI fixes ####
- New **Silenced** symbol (🤐) on units, sites and artifacts whose abilities have been removed (distinct from Disabled, which also stops it acting). Added to the Symbols legend.
- Unit status badges no longer pile on the same corner — they now sit in a neat row, so several at once (e.g. Airborne + Silenced + Ward) are all visible. Disabled now shows a clear 🚫 badge, and Silenced its 🤐, matching the Symbols legend.

#### Card fixes ####
- Lava Flow now matches its card: the flow starts **on the caster’s own site** (5 damage there, then 4, then 2 outward — the caster itself is spared, but other units sharing its site are not), instead of skipping the caster’s site and landing a square too far. And “each other unit occupying a site” now correctly includes units burrowed underground or submerged underwater on the affected sites, not just those on the surface.
- Vivien the Enchantress now also copies her sources’ *triggered* abilities that fire on your own actions — not just their activated abilities and end-of-turn draws. So if you (as Vivien) face an Enchantress, casting a spell correctly offers to animate one of your auras (e.g. Crusade), as her copied ability should. Same for other “when you cast / play a site / draw” avatar & spellcaster abilities.
