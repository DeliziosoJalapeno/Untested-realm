#### UI fixes / improvements ####
- **sealed now works in mobile** , resolving breaking bug
- **minor mobile ui fixes**: logs don't cover card panel anymore, card panel is bigger so both card and text can be displayed without scrolling in most cases
- **carried artifacts are bigger** when site is not crowded

#### Card & rules fixes ####
- **Silent Hills** now also stops the **Genesis** of a minion that enters a hushed nearby site — a silenced minion has no abilities, so nothing triggers as it arrives.
- **Gnome Hollows** blocks a 3-or-more-power minion from entering *by any means* now — not just by walking in, but also when **Blinked**, **Teleported**, or otherwise forced in.
- **Browse**, **Crossroads**, and **Apostles of Thamariel** no longer leak the cards you peek at to your opponent — the looked-at cards are kept private to you.
- **The Great Drowning of Men** no longer drowns **Submerge** minions: the rubble it floods is now properly water, so a submerged minion sinks and survives while everything else drowns.
- **Siege Giant** can hurl any weaker allied *unit* — including your own Avatar — not only minions.
- **Pathfinder** blazing a trail now respects **Magellan Globe**: at the edge of the realm it can lay its site on the wrapped-around opposite edge, like every other adjacency effect.
- **Mock Court** now actually charges its summon tax: each Ordinary token summoned costs (1). If you can afford them all you pay for all; if you can't cover a whole batch (e.g. **Plague of Frogs**' seven Frogs), you choose which to keep and the rest never arrive — instead of the unpaid ones sneaking in for free.
