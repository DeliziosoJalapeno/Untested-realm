#### Doomsday Device

* The Device now wears its **fuse count** right on the card (solid black), like the Immortal Throne's level.
* Every tick **flashes the fresh number** over it — white-bordered black, and **growing bigger** as the countdown tightens, up to a final ☢.
* At **1 tick left** it **pulses an angry red** so you can't miss the imminent detonation — on the ground **or** while it's being carried.
* When it finally goes off, the blast **lights up the full area grid** like a cast area-spell — themed an ominous **black**.
* All of the above works whether the Device is sitting on a site or riding on a minion.



#### Rules fixes

* **Overflowing Court** now correctly disables Court *minions* too (Seelie Court, Unseelie Court, Court Jester), not just the other Court *sites* — matching its "Other Courts are disabled" text.
* **Enchantress timing.** Her *"you may animate an aura"* trigger now resolves **before** the spell you cast resolves — for **any** spell type. So the freshly-animated aura minion is already on the board when a cast minion's **Genesis** fires (e.g. Death Dealer's board-wipe), when a Magic's effect goes off, or when an Artifact/Aura is placed. It was resolving a beat too late.



#### Interface

* **Site targeting fixed.** When selecting a site as a target, hovering it no longer lifts the site's art **above the minions standing on it** — the highlight brightens in place instead.
* **Doomsday Device new animation**

#### Card fixes

* **Erik's Curiosa** now draws from your **collection**, as printed (it was falling back to a spellbook draw). Ripping it lets you take a card from your collection into hand — your pick, and the opponent never learns which.
* **Arcade of Bones** now actually lets **Undead move freely** 
* **Hotwheel** rolling through a location now triggers that site's "when a unit enters/leaves here" effects — **Briar Patch** thorns, Druid, Dark Alley, and the like.
