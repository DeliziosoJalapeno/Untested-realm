2026-09-10

#### New & interface ####

* **New optional rule: 🔮 Second Seer.** A checkbox when you start a game (online, sealed, hotseat, or vs computer). With it on, the player going **second** is granted the **Seer** avatar's peek — look at the top of your spellbook or atlas and keep it or send it to the bottom — to soften the disadvantage of going second. Off by default. *(Reworked in 0.3.40 — see there.)*
* **The mulligan screen tells you if you're going first.** Your opening-hand screen now shows **🥇 You go first (Player 1)** or **🥈 You go second (Player 2)**, so you can mulligan with the turn order in mind.
* **Masked Imposter shows its real card.** When an Imposter is wearing another Avatar's face, its true **Imposter** card is now pinned small in the bottom-left corner of the chip — hover it for the card panel and its FAQ ruling.
* **Brobdingnag Bullfrog shows what it swallowed.** A minion gulped into the Bullfrog's belly is normally hidden; its card is now pinned small on the Bullfrog — hover to see (and read the FAQ of) exactly what's trapped inside.
* **Tighter keyboard-shortcut bar.** The desktop hotkey legend in the top bar was wrapping onto two rows; the spacing has been tightened so it fits on one.

#### Engine general fixes ####

* **Minions cast by another card now fire their Genesis properly — including picking its target.** When something *casts* a minion for you — **Toolbox** ("cast an Ordinary spell from your collection"), **Silver Bullet**, the **Malleus Maleficarum**, a **Chaoswish** copy, and the like — you were only asked *where* to summon it, never for its Genesis's target. So a minion whose Genesis needs a target, like **Vile Imp** ("Genesis → May deal 2 damage to target adjacent unit"), entered play and then did **nothing**. Now the effect-cast asks for the Genesis target too (measured from where the minion lands), exactly like casting it from your hand — so Vile Imp fetched with Toolbox gets to zap an adjacent unit. This fixes the whole family of "cast a minion" effects at once.

#### Card fixes ####

* **Scatter now pushes *everything* at the location — including auras and artifacts.** "Push everything at target location one step, one at a time" was only moving the units there. Now the ground artifacts and the auras get pushed too, one at a time: a single-site aura shifts to an adjacent site, a 2×2 aura slides one step to a new intersection, and a wall slides to an adjacent border. (The site itself still stays put, per the rules.)
* **Colicky Dragonettes lose Stealth when they hiccup a fireball.** Firing their end-of-turn projectile is a revealing action, so a Stealthed Colicky Dragonettes now spends its Stealth token when it shoots — just like declaring an attack or taking a Ranged shot. Before, they stayed hidden after firing.
* **Sold-out Cemetery no longer soft-locks when a summoned Undead replaces a sacrificed one.** "Whenever Undead enter this site, push another Undead here away one step" was offering the *just-sacrificed* Undead — killed a moment earlier by the same effect and not yet cleared off the board — as the one to push. Choosing it (it was the only option) pointed at a unit that then vanished, freezing the game. Dying Undead are now skipped, so the push only ever targets one that's really there.
* **The "choose your prevention order" prompt no longer lists effects that aren't actually reducing the hit.** Both damage *reductions* — Shield Maidens, Shellycoat, Ironclad, Excalibur, and an **Imposter** wearing a reducing mask — and elemental/subtype *immunities* — Sirian Templar, Ignis Rex, Lava Salamander, Sir Galahad, and the rest — are now only offered when they'd genuinely take something off *this* blow. So an Imposter with no reducing mask, an Ironclad that only shields itself when a *different* unit is hit, or an immunity against the wrong kind of attacker is no longer shown as a pointless option to order.
