2026-08-28

#### Migration from curiosa.io to sorcerytcg.com ####
- **Import decks from sorcerytcg.com.** The deck builder now imports directly from the official **sorcerytcg.com** builder — paste a `sorcerytcg.com/decks/…` link (curiosa.io, which is being retired, still works as a fallback). Avatar, spellbook, sites and sideboard all come across intact — **including each card's chosen art**.

### Card alts are now playable! ###
- **Alternative card art.** Cards with alternate printings (promos, Gothic, Arthurian Legends, Dragonlord, and the premium **Curio** arts) can now be given a different art: in the deck builder, click the **🎨** next to a card to pick its printing (the preview updates to match). Your choices are saved with the deck. 
- **your opponent sees the arts you picked** on the cards you play (only for cards they can actually see, so nothing about your unrevealed deck leaks).
- **change a card's art mid-game from the Editor** — a printing picker appears for the selected card (or an avatar) whenever it has alternatives.

#### Interface ####

- **Units walk their route.** Animation for multi-step moves, even when attacking
- **Fallen cards fade out.** Death/removal from realm animation
- **Teleports blink cards away** Teleport effects animation
- **Scorched sites are marked.** A location a roaming **Wildfire** has already burned now shows a **🔥 scorched mark** (beside the 🌊 flooded mark), so you can see at a glance where the fire has been — and where it can still spread.
- **Editor: bury / submerge / surface.** The Editor can now send a selected **minion or artifact below** (underground under a land site, underwater under a water one) or bring it back up, in place — handy for setting up subsurface scenarios. A minion sent below keeps its footing there instead of vanishing.
- **Editor: hand over a monument.** you can change control of monuments like you do for minions.
- **Monuments glow their controller's colour.** A Monument on the board now shows a **blue border when you control it and a red one when the opponent does** — just like a minion — so at a glance you can tell whose it is (and see it change hands).

#### Card fixes ####
- **Wildfire places on a single site.** Conjuring **Wildfire** now asks you to pick **one square** (it only ever covers a single site), instead of the two-by-two intersection selector the larger auras use.
- **Earthquake buries everything.** Earthquake's final tremor now **buries loose artifacts** on the affected sites too — not just minions — sending them underground exactly like Cave-In does across its four locations.
- **Blink** only lets you pick a destination **nearby the ally you're moving** now, instead of accepting any square on the board and then quietly fizzling (drawing the card but not moving the unit) when the square was too far.
- **Sites can be Spellcasters.** A site with the Spellcaster ability can now actually **cast spells from your hand**, choosing the site as the caster — which matters, since a projectile magic (Magic Missiles, Ball Lightning…) then fires **from the site's square**, not your Avatar. This makes **River of Flame** (a Fire Spellcaster) work, and powers **Merlin's Tower**.
- **Merlin's Tower** works properly now: its **Genesis** correctly checks your true **Air affinity** (bonuses like Elementalist included, not just printed site symbols), it **becomes a Spellcaster this turn**, and the **"③ less"** discount applies only to a magic **cast by the tower itself** — as the card says.
- **Doomsday Cult** is now playable on screen. While a Cult is in the realm, **the top card of each player's spellbook is shown** (📖 with a thumbnail, next to their deck counts) — yours and your opponent's — since the card reveals both. When your revealed top is an **Evil minion** you can afford, it **glows green: click it to cast it straight onto the Cult's site** (paying its cost). 
- **Cards cast from a collection go to the cemetery.** A minion, artifact or aura cast for real from your collection or deck (Silver Bullet, Toolbox, the Malleus Maleficarum, Troubled Town, the Doomsday Cult…) is now a **real card**: when it dies it lands in your **cemetery** instead of vanishing. (Free spell copies and one-off magic still resolve and disappear as before.)
- **Makeshift Barricade shields your Avatar — and breaks on total damage.** The Barricade now prevents damage to your **Avatar** standing on its square too — the Avatar is an ally "here" — not just your other minions. It also breaks correctly when the damage it soaks up **all at once totals 3+** across everything it shelters (per the ruling, 1 damage to three allies here at the same time adds to 3 and breaks it), not only on a single 3+ hit.


#### Performance ####
- **Move planning can't hang.** Picking where to move a unit no longer risks a freeze on boards with lots of free-movement sites (Updraft Ridge, Arcade of Bones), where the number of possible routes explodes. Route options for the picker are now bounded, while the set of squares you can reach and the shortest route to each stay exact.

#### Bot ####
- **The computer no longer freezes dividing damage.** When one of the computer's attackers strikes into **several defenders at once** and has to **split its damage**, it now picks a valid split instantly (it never pours more into a single minion than kills it, and — when it can wipe out every defender — just does so) instead of stalling on the choice.
