#### New chess-engine like bot ####
- **New difficulty setting: Fast vs Thinking.** On the *Play vs Computer* panel you can now pick your opponent. **Fast** is the familiar instant, casual bot. **Thinking** is a much stronger search AI that actually plans its turn — reading ahead, weighing trades, and lining up its win condition.
- new bot, inspired from chess bruteforce search algorithms, actually explores a huge number of possible actions to get the turn with the evaluated best outcome at the end of it, also accounting for multiple opponent responses. Already stronger than old bot, it's a solid engine that could be improved even more in the future with better evaluation, deeper/smarter searches and procedural additions
- new bot might be slow sometimes, hard capped to 2 minutes per turn (after that, fast bot takes control)

#### and chess like coordinates too #### 
- **coordinate system reworked**, now all coordinates are shown as column - letter / row - number (e.g. a1, b4, e3). Player specific, going from bottom left (a1), to top right (e4)

#### Editor Fixes ####
- **Discard from hand.** The Table tab now lets you send a card straight from a hand to the cemetery — pick it from the list and hit *discard*. It only appears for hands you can actually see (your own), so an opponent's hidden hand is never exposed.

#### Combat targeting & prompts ####
- **Pick exactly which enemy you attack.** When several co-located enemies share a name — a tapped and an untapped Foot Soldier, or two Horrible Hybrids on different life — the attack menu now collapses them into one **"⚔ Attack Foot Soldier (2) — pick one"** entry. Choosing it lets you **click the specific one** on the board; the candidates glow gold so you can tell which is which.
- **Clearer defender choices.** The defend prompt now labels each candidate with just enough to tell it apart
- **Combat glows.** While a defend prompt or the move/attack menu is open, the **attack target** and every **candidate target** glow gold, and the **attacker (or your mover)** glows red — so at a glance you see who's fighting whom. Hovering an option **brightens its target** and **focuses that card** in the side panel. An attacked or attackable **site** glows on its square (the same yellow highlight movement uses) and never lifts above the minions standing on it.

#### Rules fixes ####
- **flooding**, and expecially **unflooding** debugged: now losing a flooding source doesn't dry out sites if something else is still flooding them
- **deathrite** now always implies the unit not leaving the realm until it is completed, might fix some prompts.

#### Card fixes ####
- **Greeen Knight** fixed, auto-taunt effect now working
- **Ribble Boggart** no longer permanently has Ranged and Lethal. It gets **one random mutation per turn** (Airborne, Ranged, Lethal, or +3 power) that lasts until end of turn, as printed. (A parsing quirk was reading the mutation *list* in its rules text as innate keywords — a couple of other cards were quietly affected too and are fixed.)
- **Animated aura minions** (Enchantress) now die together with their enchantment on **any** exit — bury, flood, banish, exile — not only when killed with the Enchantress still around. The aura no longer lingers as a ghost.
- **Wave of Eviction** now carries enemies off the **origin** water site too, and the flood/push continues **across voids** to the edge of the realm (only real sites flood, and enemies can't be shoved into the void). It also uses the standard direction picker with the conveyor-belt animation flowing from the water site.
- **Bind Evil & Gargantula's cocoon.** The *"Tap → release / cut free"* button now appears even after the source is gone — Bind Evil is a spell that's already in the cemetery, and a Gargantula may have since died, but the bound/cocooned minion can still be freed as intended.
- **Demonic Contract** now **reveals** the card it tutors from your spellbook, as printed.
- **Saintweald** now works(it was silently failing) — a minion summoned here fires its deathrite as a genesis, including deathrites that ask you a question. **Swiven Scout** was fixed for the same underlying reason.
- **Tide Naiads** no longer logs a phantom "deathrite" it doesn't have; the water it floods now **ebbs on any exit** (death, bounce, banish), even if the Naiads were silenced.
- **Sold-out Cemetery** now resolves correctly inside a Move & Attack: when your Undead moves in, the site's controller pushes another Undead out **before** the attack — and if your declared target got pushed away, you're re-prompted to pick a new target or end your movement.
