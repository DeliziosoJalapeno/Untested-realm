2026-10-27

#### Rules fixes ####
* **Strikes first**: a unit killed by the first strike deals no damage back, even when its death waits on a Deathrite (e.g. Estranged Loner).
* **Disabled units** follow the codex (geleeroyale):
  * They have no keywords.
  * They can't tap to pay a cost: Siege Ballista, Payload Trebuchet, Corpse Catapult, Fae City, Dhol Chants.
  * Their "when struck / damaged" abilities don't trigger. Avatars are exempt.
  * They can't strike or take an action granted by another card: Shatter Strike, Raze, Trial by Water, Duel, "every unit here strikes" effects.
* **Disabled sites** (Blightstone, Overflowing Court) give no mana or affinity (geleeroyale).
* **Projectiles** from Fireball, Firebolts, Heat Ray, Bone Spear, Ice Lance, Ball Lightning, Flamecaller, Grapple Shot and Colicky Dragonettes now fly over what Magic Missiles already flew over: allies next to a Crawling Congregation, and minions swallowed by a Brobdingnag Bullfrog.

#### Card fixes ####
* **Troll Bridge** only hits a *lone* enemy, as the FAQ says. A War Horse carrying its Avatar, or any enemy entering together with another enemy, crosses for free. Enemies already standing on the bridge don't count (Jalapeno) Also, crossing two bridges in one move (Hotwheel) asks for the second toll from the hand as it is after the first one (geleeroyale).
* **Omphaloi**: their minions are summoned where the Omphalos is when you cast them, not where it was when it drew the spell (e.g. after a Cloud City flies it elsewhere). Same for Cradle of Etherrum and Vivien.
* **Atlantean Fate**'s Genesis no longer drowns minions (or buries artifacts) atop Ordinary *land* sites in its area, which it doesn't flood. Ordinary *water* sites are still submerged, as the FAQ says. Artifacts carried by a minion that gets submerged (by Atlantean Fate or any other effect) now go underwater with it.
* **Animist**: casting a magic as a Spirit is now a true cast of a *minion* spell. Cost changes apply (Maddening Bells near the Animist, Court of Equity, minion discounts like Donnybrook Inn, which also lets you summon it there). "When cast" effects trigger (Mobbed Court…), and you need the magic's threshold. The Spirit's power is still the magic's printed cost.
* **Transforming isn't entering.** Pollimorph's Frog, Degradation's Foot Soldier and an awakened Horns of Behemoth / Island Leviathan take the old card's place without "entering": no flipped-Druid thorns, no summon bans or taxes. Pollimorph and Degradation also can't transform a "can't be modified" minion.
* **Vast Desert** fixed, for some reason it missed desert standardization and kept some bugs that were already fixed on all other deserts
* **Iron Shackles**: a disabled unit has no basic abilities, so the shackled bearer can't drop the Shackles (or anything else).
* **Salt the Earth** is now correctly placed as a single-site aura, like Hamlet's Ablaze.
* **Croaking Swamp** at a water site lets you choose whether the Frog arrives on the surface or submerged (geleeroyale).
* **Pathfinder** reveals the top site of the atlas before you choose where to place it. The fake "draws a card" log at setup is gone (geleeroyale).
* **Kelp Cavern** logs the spell it moves from the bottom of your spellbook to the top (Aadu).
