##### Taming the Wildfire ##### 

- **A single-site aura follows its site when it moves.** A 1×1 aura conjured atop one site — **Wildfire**, **Castle's Ablaze!**, **Hamlet's Ablaze!** — is now carried along when that site is moved or swapped (Baba Yaga's Hut, Earthquake, Mover of Mountains…), instead of being left behind on the bare square.
- **Wildfire** only burns the surface.
- **Wildfire tracks the sites it's on, not bare squares.** Wildfire is conjured atop a **site** and now remembers where it is — and which locations it's already burned — by that **site's identity** rather than its coordinate. So if a site is **moved or relocated**, the fire travels with it (and burns whatever moved with it) instead of being left behind on an empty square, a fresh site sliding into a burned spot is correctly still burnable, and the scorched 🔥 marks follow the sites as they move.

##### Forced/Extra movement now always obeys the rules instead of teleporting #### 
A sweep of every card that moves a unit found many that quietly *teleported* it — ignoring walls, Immobile, entry-bans, the Cage of Sidrak, and "can't be pushed into the void." 
  - **"Takes a step" / "moves to an adjacent location"** respects step legality (Immobile, walls, entry-bans) — a unit that can't take the step stays put. Fixes **Innsmouth Dock**, **Archangel Michael**, **Second Wind**, **Backstab**, **Dodge Roll**, **Purge Juggernaut** and **Frontier Settlers** (an Immobile one now plays its site but can't move onto it).
  - **"Move N steps in a direction"** now walks one legal step at a time, stopping at a wall/edge and firing on-enter effects at each location. Fixes **Bull Demons of Adum**.
  - **"Push / pull / drag one step"** is now a proper forced shove: it can't drag a unit out of a **Cage of Sidrak**, can't shove anything into the **void**, and honours push-only barriers. Fixes **Windblast**, **Whirlwind**, **Maelström**, **Undertow**, **Wind Sylph**, **Sold-out Cemetery**, **Nightmare**, **Gargantula**, **Gloam Toads**, **Lacuna Entity**, **Megalurker**, **Nelly Longarms**, **Pudge Butcher**, **Ormund Harpooneers**, **Fire Harpoons!** and **Darkest Dungeon**.
  - **"Drag down" and "slide"** effects (**Grasping Ground**, **Mudslide**) now respect "can't be moved by force" and fire on-enter triggers.
  - **Joust!** now checks that both jousters can actually take their step and fires their arrival triggers.

#### "Allies" effects include your Avatar — and the unit itself ####
 Effects that buff/protect "allies" (not "*other* allies") now correctly cover your **Avatar** (an Avatar is an ally, and positive effects apply to it) and, where the wording includes the source, the unit itself. Fixes **Ruler of Thul** (its edge-row +1 reaches an allied Avatar, and the Ruler itself can wrap edge-to-edge), **Pendragon Banner**, **Death Knight**, **Fallen Angel**, **Sir Priamus**, **Old Salt Anchorman** and **Sir Galahad** (these three now also protect themselves).


#### "Here" means really "here" ####

- **A site's effects reach its underground/underwater too.** For a **site**, "here" means both its surface **and** its subsurface location — so a burrowed or submerged unit at the site is now correctly affected. Fixes **Standing Stones** (subsurface minions are Spellcasters), **Temple of Moloch** (a subsurface minion can be sacrificed), **The Geistwood** (a minion dying underground/underwater still echoes its Genesis as a Deathrite), **Dark Alley** and **Troll Bridge** (a unit that submerges/burrows *in* from outside the site counts as entering), **Pilgrim's Shrine** (a unit leaving the subsurface carries the Ward), **Planar Gate** (subsurface minions gain Voidwalk), **Free City** (can attack subsurface minions), **Fae City**,  and **Wedding Hall**. Because a site can be **flooded or dried up**, this correctly covers *both* subsurface layers.
- **Auras stay on the surface, like Wildfire.** An aura sits *atop* its sites, so its area effect no longer reaches a **burrowed or submerged** minion underneath.  A **burrowed or submerged** unit sharing Wildfire's square is no longer scorched — the fire roams on the surface. **Year of the Blaze** now consumes "everything here" on the **surface** only (a burrowed minion under an affected site survives the blaze), and **Cursed Iron** only sears a bearer standing on the surface. (The Black Plague and The Great Famine still deliberately afflict the *whole* site, per their rulings.)
- **A minion's "here" is its own layer.** For a minion, "here" is the single location it stands on — its square *and* its region — so effects that hit "units here" no longer reach a burrowed/submerged unit at a different layer of the same square (and vice-versa). Fixes **Silver Valkyries** (untap allies here), **Puppet Master** (control tapped minions here), **Asmodeus** ("destroy everything else here" — surface only, like Year of the Blaze, plus the site), **Vatn Draconis** (once submerged it no longer drags down the surface units above it), and **Sir Morien** (his "enemy projectiles can't enter here" now only stops shots travelling in his own region). Most "here" minions were already correct.
- **An artifact's "here" is its own layer too — even when buried or submerged.** An artifact (or Monument) occupies a single location, and an uncarried one can itself be **buried or submerged**, so its "here" follows *its own* region. Fixes **Crucifix** and **Scarecrow** (return/ban minions only in the artifact's own region/location), **Pendragon Banner** (a burrowed enemy under it no longer switches off the +1), **The Immortal Throne** (the "an Avatar here alone wins" check reads the Throne's own layer), **Shrine of the Dragonlord** (teleports Dragons to the Shrine's actual location) and **Rolling Boulder** (crushes along its own region). A carried artifact correctly uses its bearer's location. Likewise, a Monument that summons "here" — **Pile of Skulls** and the **Omphaloi** — now materializes the minion in the Monument's own layer, so a buried/submerged one summons below (where a non-Burrowing/Submerge minion can't survive).


#### "Empty means really "empty" ####
- Cards that care about an **empty site** — **Baba Yaga's Hut** (swap with an empty back-row site), **Pristine Paradise** (provides mana/threshold only while completely empty) and **Mover of Mountains** (swap two nearby empty sites) — used to look only for units and could wrongly treat a site as empty when it still held an **artifact** or an **aura**. A site now counts as empty only when **nothing** is on it: no units in any region, no ground artifact, no face down cards, and no aura. (Walls on the border between two sites don't count as being "on" either site.)

 ##### Other card fixes
- **Mimic** now is shown all artifacts to transform into at cast time. Fixed the bug that made it only work on your sites
- **Shield Maidens** now considers **magellan globe**
- **Sir Tom Thumb can be picked up.** A **Beast** (allied or enemy) standing on Sir Tom Thumb's square now shows a **Carry** button — "May be carried by any Beast" finally works from the board.
- **Stone-gaze Gorgons disable their own square too.** "Adjacent locations" includes the Gorgons' **own** location, so a minion standing on the Gorgons is now disabled as well (and the effect wraps across the Magellan Globe's joined edges). Still never disables the Gorgons itself or an avatar.
- **The Tower of Babel casts its free spell right away.** Building the Tower (playing **The Apex of Babel** atop **The Base of Babel**) draws a spell and lets you **cast one immediately for free** — picking it in the prompt casts it then and there (with its own target/placement prompts), instead of leaving a "free cast" credit hanging on a card in your hand.
- **Spire Lich stands tall on the Tower of Babel.** A **Spire Lich** atop a **built Tower of Babel** now correctly gains its +2 power, Ranged and Spellcaster — the merged Apex+Base counts as a Tower, just like a site named "…Tower".
- **A single-site fire rides its site when it moves.** A 1×1 aura conjured atop one site — **Wildfire**, **Castle's Ablaze!**, **Hamlet's Ablaze!** — is now carried along when that site is moved or swapped (Baba Yaga's Hut, Earthquake, Mover of Mountains…), instead of being left behind on the bare square.
- **Losing your Avatar loses the game — fringe Thaïs and Imposter interaction** Flipping an **Imposter** that's masked as the **Druid** (Druid's "Summon Bruin, flip this card") turns it into a faceless husk, leaving that player with **no Avatar** — which now correctly ends the game as a loss. You're still never offered that pointless self-defeat on your own turn, but with **Courtesan Thaïs** (you pilot the opponent's next turn) you *can* mask their Imposter as Druid from their own collection and flip it — winning the duel outright.
- **A face-down card is no longer treated as a creature.** A double-faced card flipped to a side it doesn't have (e.g. **Vivien** copying an unflipped **Druid**'s flip and flipping herself) becomes a blank, face-down husk — per the rules **no longer a minion, with no card type**. It now can't be **targeted** by spells, can't be **attacked**, and isn't a random victim for location effects like **Lightning Bolt** — but it still **occupies its square** (so it keeps a site from being "empty", e.g. **Pristine Paradise**) and is still wiped by "destroy everything" effects like **Roots of Yggdrasil**.
