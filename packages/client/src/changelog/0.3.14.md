2026-08-06

####UI fixes and improvements####
- A location holding more than 6 minions now tiles them into a progressively smaller, non-overlapping grid that fills the square (up to 8 in a 2×4, 12 in 3×4, 15 in 3×5, 18 in 3×6, and 28 in a 4×7) 
- The floating minion / site / artifact action menu can now be dragged aside on PC too (grab its title or background) — like the prompt banners — so it never traps a board square you need to click.
- You are no longer locked in place while waiting for an opponent: after creating a room or searching for a match you can browse decks, your collection and the rest of the site (a small banner brings you back, and you’re pulled in automatically when the game starts).
- When your opponent publicly reveals cards (Common Sense, Black Mass…), you now get a close-only popup showing the revealed card(s), each linking to the card detail panel.
- Keyboard hotkeys (left-hand cluster): W skip a card popup · D step the computer’s turn · S toggle subsurface · A your collection · C your cemetery · X opponent’s cemetery, and Enter to confirm the on-screen action. Toggles use the same key; ignored while typing.
- Savior: after you summon a minion, a quick banner offers to ward it (a shortcut to the same “(1) → Ward a minion summoned this turn” ability — the normal ability button is unchanged).
- Editor: you can now select any unit — the opponent’s, or your own on their turn — to resolve card text by hand, not only your own units on your own turn.

####General ruling fixes####
- A burrowed creature that also has Submerge no longer dies when its site is flooded — it simply becomes submerged (the same subsurface, now underwater). One without Submerge still drowns.
- Stealth is now broken when a minion becomes silenced or disabled (it is an ability, like Ward), and when a minion casts a spell (casting interacts with the realm) — a Stealthed Morgana le Fay is revealed when she casts.
- A card’s action menu now scrolls when it has many actions (e.g. Vivien copying lots of abilities)
- An animated aura minion (Enchantress) is now killed the instant its aura is dispelled, and if an opponent takes control of it, they keep the aura when it settles back into an enchantment.
- Caster-locked spells can only be cast by their bonded caster
- Combat strikes in a trade are now truly simultaneous: a minion that dies to your blow still counts for the enemy’s simultaneous strike 

####Card fixes####
- Plague of Frogs now summons the whole swarm — all seven Frog tokens — to a single location you choose (per the most recent FAQ), instead of asking you to place each frog separately.
- The Black Plague and The Great Famine now reach minions burrowed or submerged on an affected site, not only the ones standing on the surface — the plague afflicts the whole site.
- Locusts of Illyria: with two or more of your Locusts in play, only ONE copy spreads at end of turn (it was spreading one per Locust). A copy may still spread onto a nearby site of either player.
- Dragonlord: you now choose your set-aside Unique Dragon (from all of them) at the start of the game, and it is shown small — like a carried artifact — inside your avatar card (hidden from your opponent).
- Den of Evil and Dragonlord’s Lair now actually waive threshold as printed (Evil / Dragons cast there need no threshold), not just the (1) cost reduction.
- Black Mass, Archangel Samael and hand-reveal effects now count a Corruptor’s converted minions as Evil (your Beasts are Monsters, Mortals are Undead, Angels are Demons).
- The Omphaloi are now proper spellcaster artifacts: each casts its OWN drawn spell itself (not your avatar), and may also cast any hand spell of its element (e.g. Char Omphalos casts Air or Fire).
- Vivien the Enchantress (hopefully) fixed
- Crusade and Jihad only let you summon earth / fire minions onto the sites they actually cover, not anywhere.
- Pathfinder now triggers the Genesis of sites it plays from its atlas (they were entering the realm without their Genesis firing).
