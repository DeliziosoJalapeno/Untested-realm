2026-09-16

#### GUI Fixes ####

* **The deck builder now has a card panel on mobile.** Previously the card-detail panel was hidden entirely on phones — there was no way to read a card while building. The card list is now split: the results grid takes the top half, and a card panel sits right below it. **Tap a card to inspect it** (its full art + text appear in the panel), then tap **＋ Add** to put it in your deck or collection. When no card is selected, the panel shows the live deck analysis.
* **The masked-Imposter badge no longer hides the life bar.** The little real-Imposter card pinned on a masked avatar sat over the bottom stat bar; it's been lifted just above it so power/life stays readable.
* **Promotional & alternative site art now shows the right way up.** The promo / alt-art printings of Site cards were stored portrait (rotated), so they read sideways in the atlas and on the board. They're now rotated to landscape like every other site.
* **Some curios now show the real card, not a square crop.** Nine curios (Belfry, Deathspeaker, Dozmary Pool, Entangle Terrain, Plague of Frogs, Gift of the Frog, Murder of Crows, Sorcerer, Wyvern) display the actual card-shaped foil image, extracted from the Collector Arthouse photos. The rest keep their square art crop (no full-card image recovered for them yet).

#### Card Fixes ####

* **Masked imposter (and Vivien) can now get elementalist effect** An Imposter wearing e.g. the **Elementalist**'s face was getting none of its extra 🜁🜃🜂🜄 affinity, so threshold-gated spells stayed uncastable. The mask's affinity bonus now applies immediately while it's worn. Fixed on Vivien too
* **Wicked Witch's curse is region-aware.** "Other nearby minions have -2 power" now only affects minions in the Witch's own region (a burrowed/submerged minion isn't cursed by a surface witch, and vice-versa). While fixing this I also swept a batch of "nearby/adjacent" card effects (Wicked Witch, House Arn Bannerman, Avatar of Earth, Blink, Frost Nova, Holy Ground, Monster Hunter, Desert, Displace) and two site/disable statics onto the Magellan-Globe-aware neighbour helpers, so they wrap around the board edges correctly when the Globe is in play.
* **Hillock Basilisk / Stone-gaze Gorgons don't freeze minions mid-battle.** Their "at rest" disable no longer bites a minion that's partaking in a fight (attacker, defender, or target) until the battle is over and damage is allocated — including a Basilisk that itself moved in to defend. So a disabled defender un-freezes to strike back, then settles (and re-disables) once the fight ends.
* **Vivien copies a spellcaster's "start of each turn" ability.** Vivien has the printed abilities of every Avatar and Spellcaster in the realm — but she wasn't picking up their *start-of-each-turn* triggers (e.g. a spellcaster Crave Golem's hunt). She now inherits those too.
* **Area damage is now truly simultaneous — reductions resolve before anything dies.** An explosion (Minor/Major Explosion, Lava Flow, any area spell or ability) now deals all of its damage as one wave and only *then* removes the dead. Before, it killed victims one at a time, so a damage-reducer caught in its own blast stopped shielding the rest of it: a **Minor Explosion** on your **Ironclad** avatar (−2) standing with **Shield Maidens** (−1 to nearby allies) killed the Maidens first, and the avatar wrongly took 1. Now the Maidens shield the avatar for the same blast (avatar takes 0) and die afterward. This is general engine behaviour for every damage effect, not just explosions.
* **A disabled unit provides no damage prevention or reduction.** A silenced/disabled **Tufted Turtles** no longer ducks into its shell, and a disabled **Shield Maidens** / **Ironclad**-style reducer lends nothing to its allies — a disabled unit has no abilities. (Two pure reductions like Ironclad + Shield Maidens also no longer pop a pointless "choose your prevention order" prompt, since their order can't matter.)
* **Fowl Bones (and any damage-then-summon) no longer soft-locks Sold-out Cemetery.** When an effect killed an Undead on a **Sold-out Cemetery** and summoned another there in the same step, the site could offer the just-killed Undead as the mandatory "push out" target — then it vanished and the prompt couldn't be answered. Undead that are already doomed (dying in the current damage wave) are now skipped.
* **Every "fight" is now a real strike — so it triggers Interrogator, Lethal, and kill effects.** Cards that make units *fight* — **Lord of Lies**, **Duel**, **Joust!**, **Instigator Imp**, **Giant Shark**, **Meat Hook**, **Awakened Mummies**, **Blaze of Glory** — used to just swap raw damage, skipping all the "when a unit strikes" rules. Now both combatants genuinely strike each other, so a fight properly fires **Interrogator** ("whenever an ally strikes an enemy Avatar…"), Men of Leng, Lethal / poisonous strikes, "when this strikes / is struck", and kill triggers — exactly as a normal combat blow would.
* **No Man's Land now blocks effect summons too.** "Players can't summon minions to this site or the one in front of it" was only stopping a player from *casting* a minion there — effects that summon (Boulevard of Bones' Skeleton, reanimation, conjuring…) ignored it. Any summon to a forbidden square is now blocked.
