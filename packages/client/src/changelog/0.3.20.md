2026-08-10

#### UI Improvements ####
- **Spell-cast animation now fires for spells cast from EFFECTS too** — Silver Bullet, Archimago's echo, collection casts — not only from hand. **Summon spells** glow the sites where the minions land (Border Militia), and **any** magic shows the golden caster + spell name even when it affects nothing.
- **"X dies" now names the killer** — "…dies. (killed by Y)" (or "(killed by Y and Z)").
- **Single-site auras** (Castle's Ablaze!, Hamlet's Ablaze!) are placed by clicking ONE site and shown small in the artifact space — they were mis-drawn as a full 2×2 region.
- **Archimago's echo panel now has a Cancel button.**

#### Card & rules fixes ####
- **Kill credit overhauled to the rulebook** (who gets credit for a kill: priority 1 a strike / ability-damage, 2 a Magic that unit cast, 3 an activated/triggered ability). Fixes everything that cares who killed — **Crown of the Victor**, **Accursed Albatross**, "when this kills…" triggers.
- **Accursed Albatross** finally works: killing it — by strike, spell, or ability — curses the killer's nearby allies, even when the killer dies in the same trade.
- **Fights** (Pudge Butcher, Lord of Lies…) can be forced between **allied** units and can reach **airborne** targets — a fight isn't an attack, so those Move-and-Attack restrictions don't apply.
- **Castle's Ablaze! / Hamlet's Ablaze!** cover the single site they're conjured atop and enforce their placement rule (Castle's → an Elite/Unique site, Hamlet's → an Ordinary/Exceptional site).
- **Archimago** now lets you choose which dead magic to echo **and** which two to banish alongside it — it used to burn the first three arbitrarily.
- **Corpse Catapult**'s fling button now reflects whether there's actually a corpse to fling.
- **Snowball** now sweeps up units standing on its *starting* square too (it was skipping the origin), and never counts the caster itself.
