#### Learn to play ####
* **New: a tutorial series** — open **🎓 Learn to play** on the main menu. A coach explains one rule at a time and then has you do it yourself on a prepared board, with the real rules engine. It points at the cards and squares involved, and tells you why if you try something else.
* **15 lessons in six chapters**:
  * *First steps* — the realm and sites; mana, threshold and minions; move and attack; life, Death's Door and the death blow.
  * *On the defensive* — defending and intercepting.
  * *Keywords and regions* — Airborne and Ranged; Burrowing, Submerge and the layers under a site; Lethal, Charge and Stealth.
  * *Spells* — magic and projectiles; artifacts and auras; Spellcasters.
  * *New rules* — **traps** (playing a face-down site and springing it), the **collection**, and Disabled / Outerbound / Protected.
  * *Graduation* — a full game, Frost Mage against the coach's Lavamancer, with advice for the position in front of you.
* Lessons can be taken in any order, restarted at any point, and the list remembers which ones you've finished (on this device).

#### Puzzles ####
* **New: 🧩 Puzzles** on the main menu — "win this turn" positions in the style of the old card-magazine puzzle pages. A board, a hand, one turn; the opposing Avatar is at Death's Door, and the opponent defends as well as the rules allow.
* **Eight puzzles**, easiest first, built around projectiles and blockers, the Toolbox and the collection, both kinds of trap, forcing a defender, and the Frost Mage's ice.
* Each puzzle has hints you can reveal one at a time and a written solution that explains why the tempting line fails.
* The defender is played by a solver that searches your whole turn: if any answer stops your attack, it finds it.
