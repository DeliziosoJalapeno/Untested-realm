2026-09-30

#### New ####

* **Deck sync.** Import a deck from a curiosa or sorcerytcg link and it now remembers where it came from — hit the new **🔄 Sync** button any time to re-pull the latest version of that list, keeping your local name, art and collection settings.
* **Collection screen tidy-up.** The booster / add-cards area up top is now a fixed height (no more flicker), the card preview is larger and never scrolls, and owned-count badges are easier to read.

#### Magellan Globe — the whole board wraps now ####

* **Every "adjacent", "nearby", "X steps away" and directional effect now wraps around the edges** while a Magellan Globe is in play — exactly as the FAQ says it should. That's 40-odd cards and rules: ranged range spells (Sleep, the Explosions, Mortality, Exorcism, Boil, trebuchets, ballistae…), adjacency effects (Sir Yvain, Sir Gawain, Boulevard of Bones, Battering Ram…), forced "step toward" pulls (Coy Nixie, Maelström), Ranged shots and Balor's gaze flying around the seam, bodies of water that span the join, and even raising a **Wall** on the seam border between opposite edges. With no Globe in play nothing changes.

#### Card & Rules Fixes ####

* **Slumbering Giantess** cast where she's already disabled (onto a burrowed Root Spider, or into an area-disable) no longer sleeps forever — she was piling her own "fall asleep" on top of the outside disable and staying down even after it left. Now she's active again the moment the outside condition goes away.
* **Battlemage draws once per kill.** Kill several minions in a single attack (e.g. via a Flaming Sword's splash) and you now draw a spell for each one, not just the first.
* **Carried units can attack after riding in.** A minion carried by a War Horse into a site beside a flipped Druid took the site's damage but then found "no target" — it was being snapped back onto its mount mid-move. It now dismounts cleanly and its attack lands.
