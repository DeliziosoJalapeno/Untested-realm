2026-09-02

#### ZOOM - for pc and mobile ####

- **You can now zoom into the board.** A magnifier on just the 20-site board canvas (up to 4×), with the rest of the screen untouched. **On PC:** `+` / `−` zoom, `0` resets, the **arrow keys** pan, and **Ctrl/⌘ + mouse wheel** zooms toward the cursor; there's also a small `− 100% +` control in the board's bottom-right corner. **On mobile:** pinch with two fingers to zoom and drag with one finger to pan (a tap still selects). Scrollbars only appear once you've zoomed in — at normal zoom the board looks exactly as before. The zoom lives only in your browser (it isn't shared online and resets when you reload).

#### Interface ####

- **Activated and end-of-turn abilities now animate like a spell cast.** Using an ability lights up its source (golden glow), its site, and shows a caption — the same reveal both players already saw for spells. The caption is "<card>'s ability" for most abilities, "<card> shoots!" for projectile abilities (Ranged and the like), "<avatar> plays <site>" when the avatar plays a site, and "<avatar> draws a site" when it draws one. "At the end of your turn" triggered abilities animate too — but only when they actually do something, so an idle conditional trigger doesn't flash every turn.

- **A site's ground cards shrink and wrap when crowded — and minions never slide under them.** When a site holds many ground artifacts/auras, each card shrinks past 3 and the strip splits into **two columns** past 4. Those two columns use extra-small cards so they span barely more than one column — the strip stays narrow. The minions on that site now always reserve the strip and shrink to the space left of it (at every minion count, not just ≤4), so an untapped minion is never drawn on top of an artifact.
- **Carried artifacts scale with their carrier.** When a minion's chip shrinks because it shares the site (4+ minions, or the dense grid), the artifacts it's carrying now shrink to match instead of overflowing the smaller chip.
- **Carried artifacts are shown full-size even when several are carried.** A unit toting multiple artifacts used to shrink each thumbnail; now up to **6** carried artifacts each render as large as a single one, overlapping into a left-to-right fan along the carrier. Hovering one lifts it clear so you can read and click it.
- **Scenarios: site ownership bars follow the viewpoint.** A site's blue/red control bar is now relative to who's looking (like minions and auras), so it flips correctly when you switch perspective in **hotseat and scenarios** instead of always showing the first player as blue. Its bottom-of-site position is also kept clear: an aura's effect box now fades its **bottom** border so it doesn't blend into the bar, and in **subsurface view** the bars are lifted so they stay readable against the dimmed art (while surface-only auras fade back, since they don't reach underground).
- **Auras show whose they are — and what they are.** An aura's **card** now has a control-coloured border (blue = yours, red = the opponent's, like a minion or Monument), while its **effect area** keeps its element-coloured border/tint (with a brighter inner glow along the edge). So you can read owner and element at a glance without confusing the two. Fire and Water auras use a **deeper, bloodier red and a darker blue** so their element colour isn't mistaken for the player colours.
- **The Flame of the First Ones marks the sites it has kindled.** Like a roaming Wildfire's scorch mark, a site the Flame has already burned at now shows a small flame badge (the same 🔥, tinted more yellow). It tracks the actual sites by id — so the mark follows a site that has since moved and drops off if the site is destroyed — and, as before, the flame does nothing when it stops in a non-site void.
- **Ground cards sit above the site's glow animations.** Aura and artifact cards on a site now render above its placement/highlight/combat border glows, so they're never hidden underneath one.
- **You select an aura by its CARD, not its whole effect area.** When a spell or ability targets an aura (**Arjaro Exorcist**, the Enchantress's animate) — or when editing one — you now click the aura's **card thumbnail** to pick it, instead of anywhere in its 2×2 effect area. The area still glows to show which aura is selectable, but clicks pass through it to the sites/minions beneath.


#### General engine fixes ####
- **Units can never pick up an immovable artifact.** A **Monument** or **Automaton** can't be carried (rulebook), and the game no longer even offers the "Pick up" option for one — the board click, the artifact panel, and the unit's own pick-up list all skip them, and the engine rejects the action outright as a final backstop (Automatons were previously only stopped by the client).
- **Disabled minions lose ALL their abilities — including basic keywords.** A disabled minion has no abilities at all: it loses Airborne (falls to the ground), Ranged, Charge, Stealth, Ward, Spellcaster, Strike-first, Lethal — and even Burrowing/Submerge/Voidwalk. Since it can no longer exist underground/underwater/in the void, a disabled minion that was subsurface now drowns/suffocates (or is banished from the void). It can still be moved by force.
- **Cards that drag/push a minion into the void now work.** A push or pull can't normally shove a unit into the void — but cards whose text deliberately does so now can: **Lacuna Entity** drags its victim into the abyss it dwells in, **Into the Abyss** pulls a minion into an djacent void, and **Nightmare** pushes into the void — all with full push rules (an Immobile minion is still dragged; a non-Voidwalk victim is lost to the void, a Voidwalk one survives). **Into the Abyss** also now fires "on enter" triggers and respects move-protection instead of teleporting the minion silently.
- **Grave-hate now lets you choose which cards to banish.** **Carrion Beetles**, **Ferryman's Coin**, **Funeral Pyre** and **Pile of Skulls** used to auto-banish the *oldest* cards in the chosen cemetery. Since a cemetery is a public, unordered pile, you now **pick which cards** to banish (up to the number allowed).

#### Card fixes ####

- **A Magician's hand hides the site/spell split.** Normally an opponent can see how many cards in your hand are sites vs spells (the backs differ). A **Magician** keeps its sites in the spellbook (shared back), so against one the opponent now only learns the **total** number of cards in hand, never how many are sites.

- **The passive-buff Gifts don't need a minion — you can cast one just to draw.** **Gift of the Wolf** and **Gift of the Serpent** ("Give an allied minion +2 power / Lethal … Draw a spell") merely *modify* an existing minion and don't *target* — the minion is an optional choice. With **no allied minion** you can still cast one just for the card draw, and even with minions present you may now **skip** the buff; the draw always happens. (**Gift of the Frog** and **Gift of the Raven** still need an allied minion — Frog summons a token *to* one, and Raven makes you *choose* one — so they can't be cast with none.)
- **Deathspeaker no longer appears to lose max mana.** Its flicker ability ("banish a dead minion to summon a copy") paid the copy's cost but didn't record it as spent, so the mana widget's **total** (remaining + spent) looked like it shrank every use. The cost is now counted as spent, and the total stays put.
- **Mirror Realm now respects placement rules when it reflects a nearby site.** A Mirror Realm can only become a site its own square could legally host: it can't copy an **Edge of the World** unless it sits on a void edge, nor any placement-limited site from an illegal square. Conversely it may now *enter* wherever a copyable nearby site could — including a **Rift Valley's** pull-apart seam: it opens the land the same "special way," lands in the void, and reflects the Rift Valley (per the FAQ, as long as it ends up nearby the one it copies). "Nearby" is Magellan-aware, so joined edges count. (It's still a plain site otherwise — playable anywhere a normal site is, entering as a blank Mirror Realm when there's nothing nearby to copy.)
- **The control lords let their minions go when stopped.** **Pied Piper of Hameln** ("control all minions with 1 or less power"), **King of the Realm** ("control all Mortals") and **Returned King** ("control all Undead") now all behave the same: the minions they've claimed revert to their owners the moment the lord **dies, leaves the realm, or is silenced/disabled**, and are reclaimed once it recovers. (The Piper used to never give them back at all.)
- **Sisters of Avalon: no discard, no draw.** "Discard a spell. Draw a spell." is contingent — with **no spell in hand to discard**, the Genesis now does nothing instead of drawing anyway (FAQ).

- **Tufted Turtles' shell truly PREVENTS damage.** Its "prevent the first damage each turn" is now a real prevention, not just a reduction to 0 — so a nearby **Panpipes of Pnom** ("damage from nearby units is increased to 2") can no longer boost the prevented hit back up. Prevention is final; once the shell is spent, later hits are boosted as normal.
- **Pebbled Paths now actually denies Charge.** "Minions atop nearby sites lose Charge" now also affects a minion standing **on the Pebbled Paths itself** (nearby includes its own square) — previously only its neighbours were touched, so a Charge minion summoned directly onto it kept acting. It's also Magellan-aware now (a silenced Pebbled Paths does nothing).
- **Wildfire "must start nearby" is measured from the caster.** When a **Spellcaster** (or an Omphalos) conjures Wildfire, it may now be placed nearby *that caster* instead of always the avatar — the placement highlights and the cast validation both use the real caster.

- **A Wildfire animated by the Enchantress now travels correctly.** When the Enchantress turns a **Wildfire** into a creature, moving that creature now drags the fire with it *and* scorches the site it enters — and the end-of-turn spread no longer snaps the fire back to the site it started on. (The fire's site-anchor followed the creature only visually before.)

- **Earthquake's rearrangement is now a single step.** Instead of an engine-driven chain of "swap a pair? / again? / done?" prompts, you arrange the 2×2 in one panel (swap squares as you like, then Confirm) and the whole permutation is applied at once — immovable sites stay put and an arrangement that would strand a site is refused. Same result, far less clicking, and the computer opponent handles it cleanly.


#### BOT ####
- **The computer opponent won't loop on a pointless action.** An anti-loop ward tracks whether each action changed the CPU's evaluation; a main-phase action that left its score unchanged is **excluded from its search** for the rest of that turn, so the bot moves on to its next-best choice instead of re-taking a score-neutral action forever. Covers both the Fast and Thinking opponents (the Thinking bot's search drops such moves at its root, so it can't get stuck re-planning the same one after a timeout).
