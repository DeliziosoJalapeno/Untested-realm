2026-08-10

#### UI Improvements ####
- **Bigger minions in the void** They now have the same dimension as if they were on normal sites
- **Enter keybard shortcut fixed** Pressing Enter now fires an open confirmation dialog's affirmative button — most importantly the **Concede** prompt — so you no longer have to reach for the mouse. An open dialog takes the Enter key ahead of any confirm sitting in a background prompt.

#### Card & rules fixes ####
- **A Ward is no longer wasted on a blow that deals no damage.** A Ward breaks only when its bearer *would actually take damage* — so if another effect prevents the hit first, the Ward stays up. Most importantly this fixes **Tufted Turtles**: ducking into their shell (the first hit each turn is prevented) no longer costs them their Ward. 0-damage strikes never break a Ward either.
- **Using an activated ability now breaks Stealth**, per the normal Stealth rules — the same way attacking, firing a Ranged shot, or casting a spell already did. Most visibly, **Far East Assassin** is revealed the moment it throws a carried artifact (FAQ-confirmed).
- **Stygian Archers** now raise *one* Skeleton per kill, not two — a ranged kill was double-triggering "the fallen rises again."
- **Balor of the Evil Eye** now burns one unit at *every* location along its gaze — the surface and subsurface of sites, and the void — instead of only surface units, and it no longer stops at an empty square (FAQ-confirmed).
