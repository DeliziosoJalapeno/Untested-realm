#### Rules & card fixes ####

* **Acid Rain** now silences artifacts in its area — their activated abilities too, not just their text.
* **Infernal Legion** loses Stealth when its end-of-turn blast fires, even if the damage is prevented.
* **Flaming Sword** now splashes on a Ranged hit — a ranged shot counts as a strike.
* **Grapple Shot** fizzles if the shooter is disabled (e.g. standing over a burrowed Root Spider).
* **Lances**: holding several now adds +1 each, and all of them break.
* **Free City** now works as it should
* A **carried Avatar** is dropped on the surface when its mount burrows or submerges, instead of being dragged into an illegal region.
* **Harbinger** can no longer cast a non-Voidwalk minion onto a void portent square — the void rule wins.
* A **site spellcaster** (e.g. River of Flame) now picks which region to cast from
* An **oversized / stretched caster** chooses which of its squares a projectile or ranged blast fires from.
