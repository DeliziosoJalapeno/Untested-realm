#### Interface ####
- **Cemetery, now interactive** — cards in your cemetery that carry a usable-from-the-grave effect glow a slimy green in the cemetery viewer, with a ⚗ button to activate them right there. The cemetery widget tallies them (⚗ N) and lists them by card on hover.
- **The Immortal Throne** now wears its rank: the current level counter is printed on the card in green, and every time the throne rises a green **⬆ level n** floats up from it.
- **"Forgot to end your turn?"** — after two idle minutes on your own turn, a gentle reminder appears: dismiss it and keep playing, or end your turn right there. Any move resets the countdown, so it only nudges when you've genuinely stepped away.


#### General fixes ####
- **The void is deadly — and you can't be shoved into it.** A push or pull (Scatter, Windblast) can no longer force a unit into the void: its "one step" must land on a real location. But a minion that still ends up in the void — slid in by Mudslide, or stranded when its site is banished out from under it — is immediately banished unless it has Voidwalk. An Avatar can never be forced into the void at all; and an established Avatar stranded there must play a site from hand beneath itself on its turn.
- more card effects get blocked by ward
- **Return to Nature** and other return-to-deck effects (Pendragon Legacy, Cast into Exile, Lady of the Lake) now shuffle each card into its **owner's** deck — never an opponent's, and regardless of whose cemetery held it or who controlled it.


#### Vivien the Enchantress — truly "wherever she is" ####
Vivien copies the printed abilities of **every** Avatar and Spellcaster in the realm — not just their activated powers — and she keeps them no matter which zone she's in:
- **Genesis** — if a Spellcaster with a Genesis (an Apprentice Wizard, a Great Old One…) is in the realm when Vivien enters, she fires it too.
- **Deathrite** — she performs her sources' death-rites. Copy a Nosferatu and she re-forms underground when she dies — but, unlike him, perishes there unless something has lent her Burrowing.
- **From your hand** — a Moon Clan Werewolf Spellcaster lets you sacrifice a Mortal to summon Vivien straight out of hand.
- **From your cemetery** — a Bone Rabble, Scourge Zombies, or Grigori Rasputin Spellcaster can raise Vivien from the dead, exactly as if she were them.
- **From your spellbook** — a revealed Vivien gains The Inquisition's ambush.
- **Cast restrictions** — a Lugbog Cat Spellcaster confines even Vivien to water sites (a detrimental ability is still an ability!).
- She becomes a Spellcaster from **any** Spellcaster in the realm — including a minion that merely *gained* the keyword (Wiccan Tools and friends).
- A card's **own name now means "this card"** everywhere, so every "summon [self]" ability revives whichever card actually holds it — the linchpin that makes all of the above work.
- **Activated abilities from the grave** — from your cemetery she can use a realm Spellcaster's ability that needs no body on the board (a Savior's ward, a Seer's foresight), but not one that does (a Necromancer's "summon a Skeleton *here*", an Enchantress's aura-animation).

#### magellan globe - truly connecting the realm ####
- **Wave of Eviction**, **Waveshaper**, **Vesper Swarm**, **Regurgitator**, **Interrogator**, **Pathfinder**, **Castle Haunt**, **Falling Star**, **Dream-Quest**, **Grigori Rasputin**, **Guardian Angel**, **Symmetric Suffering**, **Flame of the First Ones** now work under magellan globe
- **Mountain Giant** and other oversized 2x2 creatures edge-wrap under Magellan Globe (it can straddle the seam, and falls safely to one side if the Globe leaves).

#### other card fixes ####
- **Deathspeaker** — the raise-a-minion ability now checks **both** cemeteries live, so it lights up the instant any minion dies into either graveyard, and greys out only when both are truly empty.
- **Sea Raider** now makes *its own* controller discard and cast a spell (not the opponent's), and still triggers when it dies in the same trade.
- **Seirawan Hydra** heals only damage that *doesn't* kill it — 6 at once, or two simultaneous strikes (two Bosk Trolls), now finish it.
- **Cage of Sidrak** actually cages: locked minions are carried by the Cage, ride with it, stay tapped and disabled, and only a Teleport — never a push like River Rapids — can free them.
- **Perilous Bridge** — a pull or grapple can no longer drag a grounded unit across the top border; only a genuine Teleport may cross.
- **Vaults of Zul** loots only when an Avatar *stops* on it, and just once per game.
- **Sturmgeist** reacts only to enemy *units* casting Magic (not artifacts or sites) and steps toward the real caster.
- **Troll Bridge** correctly springs on a lone entering enemy — even if another enemy already stands there, or one is carried in.
- **Erik's Curiosa** now lets you draw a *chosen card from your collection*.
- **Day of Judgment** banishes all Evil, then damage
- **Algor Omphalos** casts must match its elements
- **Ball Lightning**: bounce-backs can hit the caster/allies but not re-hit the same unit
- **Battlemage**: no draw when it *intercepts*
- **Enchantress**: an animated enemy aura stays the opponent's 
- **King Arthur** also reclaims stolen sites and relics
- **Mask of Mayhem** doubles by the *struck* unit's position
- **Stitched Abomination**: parts destroyed by their power
- **Squirming Mass** gains current power
- **The Manor at Daperyll Hill** heals only if the full damage lands
- **The Rack**: the stretched Avatar truly *enters* the new location
-  **Replication**:conjure the copy anywhere
- **Sphere of Animosity** now truly holds an Avatar. A trapped Avatar can't leave the sphere — neither by moving nor dragged out by force (a pull, push, teleport or swap) — and still can't heal or be defended while inside. Moving between the sphere's own locations is fine.
- **Pathfinder** blazes its trail correctly under a restriction: it always plays the new site, but if it can't then move onto it — held fast by a Sphere of Animosity, or immobilised by a freshly-laid Quagmire — the site stays in play and the Pathfinder holds its ground.
- **The Omphaloi** (Dank Omphalos and its kin) can once again cast spells that shoot from the caster — Magic Missiles and other projectiles now launch from the Omphalos itself instead of erroring out.
- **Eclipse** uniformed to crusade and jihad, dynamic evil evaluation
- **Sir Mordred** — his Genesis and Deathrite now work with enemies in same square
