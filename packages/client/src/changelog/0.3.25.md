2026-08-11
#### Card & rules fixes ####
- **Adept Illusionist** — when its ability summons another Adept Illusionist, **you now choose which nearby site** it appears on, instead of it auto-spawning on the caster's own square.
- **Rubble** is now properly **controlled by no one** (including rubble placed via the Editor), and a **site can be built over rubble** again by the normal avatar site-play — the Editor used to make "Rubble" as a controlled, un-buildable-over site.

#### Pathfinder ####
- **Easier trail-blazing.** With Pathfinder selected, its adjacent buildable squares light up. Click an adjacent **void** and it blazes there **immediately**; click an adjacent **rubble** and it **asks first**, then builds a fresh site over it. Its ability can now target rubble as well as empty voids.

#### UI ####
- **Brighter prompt highlighting.** The "choose a square" highlight (summon-here, place-here, and similar prompts) now glows like the movement highlight instead of a faint outline — much easier to see.
