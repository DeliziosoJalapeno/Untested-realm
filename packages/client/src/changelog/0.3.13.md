2026-08-06

- Deck import: the "Apply text to deck" button (was "Parse text into current deck") no longer wipes a loaded deck to empty — it applies the text shown and keeps the same deck.
- Effect strikes (Hotwheel, Grapple Shot, Wraetannis Titan, Maelström…) now correctly fire "when this strikes" abilities. Striking an enemy Avatar triggers Interrogator (draw a spell unless they pay 3 life) and Men of Leng (that Avatar discards a random card).
- Stygian Archers now summon their Skeleton even when they die in the same fight they scored the kill (a trade).
- Mismanaged Mortuary now also swaps NEW cards reaching a cemetery — resolved spells, discards and mills — not just the cards already there.
- Whirling Blades (and every "strike each enemy" effect) now strikes all its targets simultaneously, per the FAQ; a unit on the path is struck once even if you step back over it, and Airborne allies may step diagonally.
- Poison Nova no longer harms magic-immune units (Failed Mutation).
- Rebecks now grants Charge to itself (it is a nearby allied Giant).
- Lord of Lies can no longer pick itself as one of the two fighting units (FAQ).
- The computer opponent no longer freezes on tricky prompts (Legion of Gall, Earthquake) — it always resolves a prompt to a legal answer, even if the play is subpar.
- Moving a unit more than one step now lets you choose the route when more than one path reaches the destination (it matters for effects like Giant Shark and Root Spider).
- An animated aura minion (Enchantress) now dies if it is taken off the surface — burrowed underground, submerged/flooded, or pulled into a void — since its aura cannot exist there.
- Forcibly burrowing/submerging an oversized unit (Bury, Drown, Cave-In, floods) now fails unless its whole footprint is one terrain (all land, or all water), per the rules.
- Auras now trigger their end-of-turn abilities — Wildfire again burns each unit under it and spreads to an adjacent site (it was doing nothing). Other end-of-turn auras are fixed too.
- Overlapping auras are now fanned out so a newly cast aura no longer completely hides one already on the same square.
- Enchantress: choose which aura to animate by clicking it on the board (highlighted), instead of picking from a text list.
- Online: a pending undo or Editor request is no longer lost when you switch tabs or briefly disconnect — it reappears on reconnect, and Editor access you were granted is likewise restored.
- On PC, prompt banners can now be dragged aside (drag anywhere on the banner) — just like on mobile — so they no longer cover a board square you need to click.
