2026-10-22

#### Rules fixes####
* Fixed uncarried carriable artifacts still being controlled by their owner.
* **"A dead minion" means a minion in either cemetery.** Fixed for **Clairvoyant**, **Book of the Dead**, **Corpse Explosion**, **Orpheus' Crossing**, **Ultimate Horror** and **Vengeful Heir** (they only looked in yours). Choices show whose cemetery each card is in.
* A **magic** goes to the cemetery only once it has **fully resolved**: **Return to Nature** can no longer return itself.
* **Artifacts** buried under a site become underwater when it floods, and underground again when it dries.

#### Card rulings & bug fixes ####
* **Vivien the Enchantress** in your hand, cemetery or spellbook only has the abilities that work from there (like Grigori Rasputin's). She no longer uses realm abilities from the cemetery, such as Seer's peek, Savior's ward or Merlin's foresee. That was really painful to implement since it nerfs my favourite card but I don't make the rules
* **Bureau of Occult Control** now charges its (2) for every effect that takes cards out of a cemetery or collection, yours included: once per effect, only when something is actually taken, and declining an optional effect costs nothing. If you can't pay, that part is skipped and the rest of the effect still happens.
* **Ultimate Horror**: you choose the order the dead Voidwalk minions rise in. Another dead Ultimate Horror rises too.
* **Pendragon Legacy**: you choose which Unique cards to shuffle back ("any number"), instead of all of them.
* **Kairos the Archivist**: the restore isn't a player accessing a cemetery, so Wormelow Tump and the Bureau don't affect it. It also no longer leaves a restored card in a sealed cemetery at the same time.
* **Blink** on an oversized ally: first pick which of its squares the teleport starts from, then a location nearby that square, then the rotation. Before, only locations nearby its top-left square were allowed.
* **Atlantean Fate** and **Flood** flood sites played under them later, not only the ones there when they were cast. Removing Atlantean Fate no longer dries a site something else flooded.
* **Aethermoeba**: a moeba spread from the void onto a site can be attacked on that site ("target was moved away" before).
* **Gargantula**: its drag doesn't say "target", so Ward and Stealth don't stop it (Aadu).
* **Omphaloi** aren't minions: Root Spider and "silence minions" effects no longer shut them off.
* **Waveshaper** checks the minions' current Submerge, not just the printed one.
* **Ferryman's Coin**: banishing cards from a cemetery works again, with clearer wording.
* Casting from your collection (**Elder Ruins**, **Troubled Town**…) checks you can pay the spell *and* the Bureau's (2) before anything happens.

#### BOT and engine speedups####
* The thinking Computer is about **1.8x faster**: it remembers positions it already evaluated and checks keywords faster. Same moves, less waiting.

#### Site ####
* Announcements: a 📢 banner can now show a message to everyone on the site (restarts, events…).
