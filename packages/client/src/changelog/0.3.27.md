2026-08-14
#### Magellan Globe — now fully connects the realm ####
- **Every edge effect wraps now.** With the Globe in play, the opposite edges of the realm are truly joined:
  - **Airborne** units fly diagonally across a corner — one step from any corner to the opposite corner.
  - **Adjacent / nearby** targeting reaches across the edge (a far-edge site is now an "adjacent" target, and the board highlights it).
  - **Direction arrows, Snowball, and projectiles** (Ranged shots and spell projectiles) continue off one edge and onto the other, in the same region.
  - **2×2 area spells** — Earthquake, Corpse Explosion, Whirlwind — can be anchored on the edge, wrapping their area to the far side.
  - **Damage-grid spells** (Lava Flow, Cone of Flame, Firebreathing, Craterize…) span the edges.
  - **Oversized minions** (Mountain Giant) can be summoned straddling the edge, their 2×2 footprint wrapping around.

#### Combat & rules fixes ####
- **Panpipes of Pnom** now raises a **0-power strike** to 2 damage (per the FAQ), while a genuine 0-damage hit still never breaks a Ward.
- **Forced steps you cause are your choice.** When you force an enemy to take a step (Coy Nixie, Guile Sirens, Abaddon Succubus, Lure, Mesmer Demon), **you** pick the direction — not your opponent. Airborne enemies may step diagonally; Immobile enemies are immune.
- **"Nearby / adjacent" effects are region-locked.** A unit-source effect targeting a nearby/adjacent unit stays in its own region unless the card says otherwise (so a surface Gargantula can't cocoon a burrowed minion). **Failed Mutation** remains immune only to *magic* — minion effects still reach it.
- **Ward stops targeting abilities too.** A triggered or activated ability that *targets* an enemy (Tooth Faeries' start-of-turn tap, and any similar effect) now breaks the target's **Ward** and is absorbed — the ability never touches the warded minion — exactly as an enemy spell already did.
- **"At rest" disablers let attackers through.** **Hillock Basilisk** and **Stone-gaze Gorgons** disable minions *at rest*, so a unit that moves into their zone to attack (the disabler itself or an ally there) now completes its attack, and is disabled only once it comes to rest. A minion **summoned** into the zone likewise fires its Genesis before falling under the gaze.
- **No card can kill an Avatar.** "Kill" and "destroy" effects simply do nothing to an Avatar — including a Mephistopheles that has become one.
- **Led Astray** now moves **avatars** (an enemy avatar is an enemy).
- **The Druid's** flip-side thorns now fire when an enemy is **forced** onto a nearby allied site (teleport, pull, Call of the Sea), not only when it walks in.
- **Blaze** — the fire trail no longer double-hits a revisited square, no longer burns the runner itself, and counts as Fire *magic* (so Failed Mutation is spared).
- **Discards obey the graveyard cards.** Discarded, milled, dispelled and spent cards now route correctly through **Kor Crematory** (banished at 4 Fire) and **Mismanaged Mortuary** (filed into the opponent's cemetery).

- **Artifacts** are now **buried / submerged** with their bearer (or when their site caves in) and show a ⛏/🌊 badge; a surface unit can't pick up a subsurface artifact, and a buried artifact stays buried when its bearer dies.

#### Card fixes ####
- **Swap** can now target a **carried** artifact (it leaves the bearer and relocates).
- **Archimago** — the echoed spell must pay its mana and thresholds (no more free copy).
- **Flanking Maneuver** lands the moved units in the destination's region.
- **The Flood** saves only one creature and keeps the whole realm flooded (rubble and later sites too).
- **Black Mass** draws three *different* minions.
- **The Doom of Dilmun** can be killed by a **sacrifice**.
- **Blightstone** stops disabling its site while silenced.
- **Atlantean Fate** un-floods its sites when the aura leaves play.
- **Drought** dries "provides for everyone" water sites too.
- **Gift of the Raven** — lifelink stacks (two casts heal twice).
- **Order of the White Wing** no longer banishes a **transformed** minion — a site that wakes into a creature (**Horns of Behemoth**, **Island Leviathan**) wasn't "summoned", so it passes through unharmed.
- **Deathspeaker** now sees minions in **either** cemetery (yours or the opponent's), fixing the false "no minions to raise" report.
- **Skeleton Mage** now **casts** the reclaimed spell (targets and all) instead of handing you a copy.
- **Chain of Prometheus** re-triggers on **every** card you draw during your turn, not just the first.
- **Slumbering Giantess** entering already disabled by another source no longer falls asleep — lifting that disable makes her fully active, no damage required.
- **Gnome Hollows** correctly refuses minions with **3+ power**, and **Leap Attack** works with **avatars**, not only minions.
