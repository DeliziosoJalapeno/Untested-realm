2026-09-17

#### Github is live! Code fully open sourced ####
* **go see the code, or just leave me a star** at https://github.com/DeliziosoJalapeno/Untested-realm

#### New ####

* **Add cards to your collection directly.** The Collection screen (where you open boosters) now has the deck builder's full card search + filters on the left — click any card to add a copy to your collection, and click one of your owned cards to remove a copy. No more relying only on booster luck.
* **Editor: replace a site in one click.** The editor's Site tools have a new **🔄 replace…** button that opens a searchable picker and swaps the selected site for any other site in the same square — keeping the same controller, with no rubble left behind.

#### GUI & Rules Fixes ####

* **Multi-step movement no longer strands your unit on a revisit.** Per the rulebook, a unit may pass over the same square more than once during a multi-step move (as long as each step is different); the manual walker now respects that instead of quietly stopping partway. (You still can't repeat the exact same step — e.g. shuffling back and forth over one edge — which is the one movement restriction the rules impose.)

#### Card Fixes ####
* **Sold out cemetery** fixed again, visually tested. Only last standing 'bug' is that it triggers at the end of the whole movement even if multi-step, because infrastructure to stop a movement with prompts isn't fully there yet. Only relevant for ultra-fringe interactions, will be fixed.
* **"Cast from your collection" now targets like a normal cast.** Silver Bullet, Toolbox, the Malleus and friends fetch a spell from your collection and cast it — and now that cast offers the *same* choices a hand cast does: you can give a fetched **artifact** to your Avatar even while it stands on an opponent's site, minions and auras get their proper region and 2×2 placement markers, and you pick which layer a minion summons to. A paid collection cast is also labelled by its real name now, not "Copy of X" (only genuinely free copies, like Chaoswish, still read "copy").
* **Saracen Raiders / Scout cast like normal minions.** "May be cast to any site without enemies" correctly *adds* locations — you can still summon them onto your own sites as always, plus onto any enemy-free site anywhere. It no longer wrongly stopped you from casting them on your own sites.
* **Fowl Bones & Bone Jumble no longer break Sold-out Cemetery.** Clicking a Skeleton on a Sold-out Cemetery to raise a bone-raiser now sacrifices that Skeleton *before* the raised minion arrives — so the site no longer offers the just-sacrificed Skeleton as a mandatory "push out" target that then vanishes and freezes the game.
* **Drums of Doom only affects its own region.** Its "nearby damage is lethal" no longer reaches a minion burrowed underground or submerged underwater beneath a nearby site — only minions on the Drums' own layer.
