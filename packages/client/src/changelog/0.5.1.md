2026-10-16
#### Performance Hotfixes####
* **Solved/mitigated 0.5.0 slowdown**: fixing disable sources possibly disabling each other uncapped previously capped calculation, resulting in exponential slowdown. Resolving an action on a crowded board (a minion entering play, a defense declared with many units out) could take **seconds** and grew worse with every extra unit. Two independent causes were fixed: the disabled-check no longer recurses through the whole board for units that can't disable anything (thanks to **aadu-gm** for the fix and the diagnosis), and the movement/reachability search now computes each unit's state **once per search** instead of re-scanning every unit on every step. A worst-case defend that took ~10s is now ~0.1s and no longer blows up as the board fills.

#### Card rulings & bug fixes ####
* **Garden of Eden** now correctly caps **caster-locked draws**. A **Morgana le Fay** (or an **Omphalos**, or **Archangel Gabriel**) drawing her private hand counts against the "one spell per turn" limit (codex FAQ), so under a Garden she draws only **one** spell — or none if you've already drawn a spell this turn. (Previously those draws slipped past the Garden entirely.)
* **Sir Balin** now burrows **carried** artifacts, not just loose ones — when his dolorous stroke destroys an Avatar's site, an artifact carried by anyone there goes underground too (same burrow as **Cave-In**). The site is destroyed **first**, then the ruins are burrowed; if the destruction leaves **flooded** ruins (under **The Flood**), nothing can burrow into the water and the effect simply fails. (The Flood now also drowns the rubble a destroyed site leaves behind.)
* **Dragonlord** now gains a Dragon's **triggered** abilities too, not just its activated ones. Taking **Adtonitum**'s aspect, the Dragonlord actually deals her "whenever she stops, 3 damage to another unit here" — and any prompts a copied Dragon ability opens now resolve correctly.
* **Dragonlord** taking the aspect of a **Voidwalking** Dragon (**Caelestis**) still **can't enter the void** — an Avatar only ever exists on the surface, so it keeps the keyword but can't step off into a void square.
* **Mana now follows control immediately, both ways** (July/Oct 2026 rules). A mana source *gained* mid-turn provides its mana **at once**, not next turn: casting **Abundance** over sites you already control hands you the extra mana now, and a site placed from the **Editor** (a Sinkhole, say) starts providing straight away. (Losing a source mid-turn already reclaimed its mana.)
* **Waveshaper** fixed two ways. "Your body of water" is the connected body your **Avatar stands in**, which is **controller-independent** — an opponent's water tile in the same lake extends it, so you can flood sites near it. And it can now target **any** nearby site — dry or already flooded — to wash over the minions standing there (per the FAQ).



#### Computer opponent ####
* **Pathfinder (and masked Imposter) computer players** are now punished hard for wasting a turn — whenever the Pathfinder *can* blaze a trail (play its topmost atlas site to an adjacent square and move there) it is strongly pushed to do so, instead of idling or wandering. (It was exempt from the normal "develop a site each turn" discipline and could dawdle.)
* The computer no longer **under-values attacking sites**. Its site-threat read now counts a **tapped** attacker (it untaps next turn), so a minion that razes a site — and taps on it — keeps the threat it still poses instead of "losing" it, which had quietly biased the bot toward merely threatening a site rather than striking it.
