2026-10-19

#### New: Precon mode ####
* Play the **12 official precon decks**: the four elemental decks from **Alpha** and from **Beta** (Sparkmage, Geomancer, Flamecaller, Waveshaper), and the four **Gothic** decks (Necromancer, Harbinger, Persecutor, Savior).
* Turn it on with **🃏 Precon mode** in *Play vs Computer*. Each player picks a precon, against the Computer or in hotseat.
* Online, tick **🃏 precon** when creating a room. Both players must bring an official precon, unedited; rooms show as *🃏 Precon* in the lobby. *Find a battle* stays Constructed.

#### New: save scenarios from online games and replays ####
* A **💾 Scenario** button saves the current board in **online games** and **replays**. A replay saves the full board at the step you're watching.
* Online saves capture the whole game, including both hands and decks. A scenario saved **mid-game stays hidden — even from you — until that game ends**, so it can't be used to peek.

#### UI ####
* **Opponent's cards upside-down** (⚙ Settings): the opponent's cards on the board face them, like across a real table. Stats and the card preview panel stay upright. This is not the default option as it gives me headache but I guess lots of you might like it
* **Spectators' chat** now shows their name (e.g. *👁 Bob*) instead of just "spectator".

#### Engine: every rule is enforced by the engine: fixes bot + third party clients potential bug ####
* The Computer could cast **Minor Explosion** on an underground location from the surface. The screen never offered that, but the engine didn't refuse it. A "target location" spell now only reaches the caster's own region.
* An audit found more rules that only the screen enforced. The engine now refuses all of these, from any client:
  * answers to a choice that weren't offered: damage allocated to a unit outside the fight, a shot hitting a unit it never reached, defending or intercepting with a unit that can't, a square or card that wasn't on the list;
  * a cast region, firing square or teleport placement the caster can't use;
  * extra or off-board targets, and artifacts conjured underground;
  * returning the same card twice in a mulligan, or picking up the same artifact twice.
* **Nearby** means the same region: **Blink** can no longer teleport a surface ally to a location underground.
* Malformed actions (a missing path, a number where a card should be…) are refused instead of crashing the engine.

#### Card rulings & bug fixes ####
* **Dread Thicket**: "the first attack *out of* this site" means a unit that takes one step away from the Thicket and then attacks (FAQ). Before, the attack made *on* the Thicket was the undefendable one.
* **Ice Shards**: each of the three shards is aimed separately (unlike Magic Missiles, it doesn't say "in the same direction"). You pick a direction for the first when casting, then aim the second and third. Each still melts after two steps.

#### BOT ####
* BOT never plays a site on the square of an enemy **Realm-Eater**, which would just eat it. If that's the only open square in its home area (6 sites near the avatar starting location), it builds outside its home instead.
