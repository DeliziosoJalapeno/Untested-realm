2026-08-28

#### A big refactoring under the hood #### 
- Now every card with custom script has its file, unpacking some sloppy thousand line files

#### Interface ####
- **Clearer same-square attacks.** Select one of your ready minions and every enemy sharing its square now **glows gold as an attack target**, with an **"⚔ Attack …" tooltip** on hover — no more guessing whether a click will fight or just move.
- **No dead-end prompts on summoning-sick minions.** A minion that entered this turn (or is tapped/disabled) can't move or attack, so selecting it no longer paints move highlights, offers a Shoot button, or lets you click an enemy to "attack" — actions the rules would only reject anyway.

#### Rules fixes ####
- **Simultaneous Avatar death is now a draw.** If a single blow fells **both** Avatars at once (e.g. a Craterize hitting two Avatars at death's door), the game correctly ends in a **draw** instead of arbitrarily handing the win to whichever death the engine happened to process first.

#### Card fixes ####
- **Khamaseen Mummy** When the Mummy surfaced next to a damage dealing **Desert** and triggered its Genesis, it doesn't crash the game anymore
- **Draconian Bonekite** targets nearby squares only
- **Single-square auras are interactive again.** Auras that cover just one site (e.g. **Wildfire**) render as a small thumbnail on that site — but had lost their board interactions. You can once more **click one to animate it** (Enchantress) and **select it in the Editor**.
