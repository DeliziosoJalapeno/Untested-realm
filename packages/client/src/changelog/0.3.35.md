2026-09-01

#### Online ####
- **Rooms clean themselves up.** Any online room that has been alive for **more than 12 hours** is now closed automatically — a finished game left open, a room that was created but never started, or an untimed game that stalled. Anyone still connected is told the room closed, so the lobby doesn't fill up with dead rooms.

#### Card fixes ####
- **Corruptor makes your Beasts count as Evil everywhere.** With a **Corruptor** avatar (your Beasts are Monsters, Mortals are Undead, Angels are Demons), those cards are now treated as **Evil** by every effect that cares about it — **Den of Evil**'s discount, **Eclipse**, **Mephistopheles**, the **Doomsday Cult**, **Howl from Beyond**, **Carrionette** and **Redmane Hyena** — not just by their printed types.
- **Swap targets only the caster's region.** **Swap** now offers only minions and artifacts in the **same region as the caster** (surface with surface, underground with underground…), as a targeted spell must — instead of letting you swap something across regions you can't legally target.
- **Giant Shark swims to its prey.** The Shark now **actually swims the shortest path through the water** to the unit it's hunting — one step at a time — instead of teleporting. It stays on **water sites** (it's Waterbound): if there's **no water route** to the prey (an Iceberg blocks it, or the prey is across dry land), it **doesn't move and doesn't fight**. Whenever more than one shortest route is possible — two, three or more — it asks you **once, up front, which whole route to take** (the choices only ever include routes that actually work — no land, no blocked steps). It also no longer reacts to a unit that merely **surfaces or submerges in the same site** (that isn't moving "between sites").
- **Forced "take a step" effects respect what can actually move.** When a card makes minions **step** (rather than teleport), a unit that **can't take the step** — Immobile, or blocked by a wall or an entry ban — now stays where it is instead of being dragged along. This fixes **Call of the Sea**, **Led Astray** and **Hotwheel** (which now also stops rolling when a wall blocks its path, not only at the board's edge).
- **Airborne diagonal steps are surface-only.** An **Airborne** unit may only step **diagonally when it starts the step on the surface** — starting in the void or underground/underwater it moves orthogonally, as the rules intend.
