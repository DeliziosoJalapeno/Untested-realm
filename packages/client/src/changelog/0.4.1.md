2026-10-04

#### UI improvements ####

* **Settings menu/ 'opponent played' popup optional** A ⚙ Settings button (home screen and the in-game buttons) collects your preferences in one place. For now: sound effects on/off, and whether the big "opponent played a card" popup appears — it's **off by default** now (the card still slides into the side panel), toggle it back on if you liked it.
* **Popups stay on the board.** Any popup shown during a game that would spawn partly off the board grid is now nudged fully inside it.

#### Card Fixes ####

* **"Up to two steps away" means two steps.** Minor Explosion, Exorcism, Mortality, Dispel and Disenchant measured range as a king-move (diagonals counted as one), so they could reach a location that's actually three steps away. They now count real steps.
* **Failed mutation now immune to projectile magic** A minion that "can't be targeted or damaged by magic" (e.g. a Failed Mutation) is no longer hit by magic projectiles like Firebolts or Fireball — the shot flies right past it. Non-magic shots (Ranged minions, abilities) still hit it, as they should.
