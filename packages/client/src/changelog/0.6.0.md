2026-11-03

#### Frost mage vs Lavamancer ####
* **Frost Mage vs Lavamancer** (spoiled, out November 13) is playable: all 36 spoiled cards, with their art. They're in the deck builder under the "frost mage vs lavamancer" set tag.
  * **The Avatars:** the **Frost Mage** encases a nearby minion in ice: it's disabled and takes no damage until the Frost Mage's next turn. The **Lavamancer** pulls apart a partial row or column, the way Rift Valley does, and fills the gap with Rubble that deals 1 damage to the minions near it.
  * **Precon mode** has early versions of both decks: **Frost Mage (previews)** and **Lavamancer (previews)**. They use the official card lists, which give no counts yet, so each deck has 3 copies of every Ordinary, 2 of every Exceptional and 1 of every Elite or Unique. Their collection cards are 1 copy each. Cards whose text hasn't been revealed are left out for now.
  * **Traps**, the set's new mechanic, follow the October 2026 rulebook. You play a Trap site face down, and everyone sees only the basic site of its element (Spire, Stream, Valley or Wasteland). It works exactly like that site until you spring it, on your turn, by paying its Trap cost. Your opponent never learns which Trap it is until then. A Trap springs once; a silenced or disabled Trap can't be sprung, and **Trapper's Rest** can reactivate a Trap that's already face up, for (1) less.
  * The new traps: Spike Trap, Volcanic Eruption, Icicle Dome, Springstem Meadow, Deep Snow, Combusting Busts, Sulfur Vents, Avalanche, Supervolcano and Bouldermaw. A trap can be sprung even when it has nothing to hit, except one whose text says "target", which needs a legal target.
  * The other new sites: Smoldering Hollow, **Scoria** and **Tundra** (your two elements become equal to the higher of the two), and Trapper's Rest.
  * **Minions:** The White Death (Lethal while Stealthed, its shot from Stealth included), Aria Wraith, Lava Elemental, Frost Elemental, Lone Wolf, Namira Cheetah, Southpaw Dyrodor (its tail swings from its controller's left), Pass-plug Serpent, Snapjaw Raptor, Stiff-pitch Witch, Emberleaf Dryad, and **Flat Realm Society**, which stops every edge of the realm from being connected, crossed or shifted (Magellan Globe, Polar Bears, Polar Explorers, Ruler of Thul, Aino, Polar Shift).
  * **Magic and auras:** Frostbolt, Ground Slam, Shatter, Polar Shift, Ash of Effort and Rite of Eruption.
  * **Fireball 2** and **Polar Bears 2** are the new set's versions of Fireball (now a plus-shaped explosion) and Polar Bears (now 3 mana, 3/3). They're separate cards: Fireball and Polar Bears keep their current text until the set is out.

#### Interface ####
* **Very wide monitors:** on a computer screen more than 2.2 times as wide as it is tall, the game no longer stretches across the whole monitor. The extra width at the sides is left empty. Ordinary widescreen monitors look the same as before.

#### Rules fixes ####
* **Forced movement rework** (geleeroyale): a unit's own step is now told apart from forced movement, as the Codex does.
  * **Lures and "take a step"** (Lure, Led Astray, Coy Nixie, Guile Sirens, Screamer, Kite Archer, Questing Beast, Leap Attack, Crave Golem, Persecutor and more) make the unit take its own legal step. An Immobile unit stays, while "can't be moved by force" and Old Salt Anchorman don't stop it (FAQ). Airborne, Voidwalk and Burrowing steps are offered where the unit has them, and "closest" counts the unit's own steps.
  * **"Can't be moved by force"** (Talamh Dreig, Iron Man Talus) now stops every forced move, its own controller's included. Being carried, buried, drowned or submerged counts as forced movement, and the Cage of Sidrak holds a minion against being forced under.
  * **Push, pull and drag:** only a real "target" breaks a Ward or skips Stealth. A projectile hit or "a unit here" doesn't. Monuments are never pushed, a carried unit takes one step however it's blown, and "when it arrives" effects need the unit to actually arrive.
  * **Teleports** stay in the unit's region and never cross into the void from a site (Blink, Flanking Maneuver, Thundering Giant, Displace, Wills-o'-the-Wisp…).
* **A forced fight** ends a Stealthed fighter's Stealth, like any fight.
* **Artifacts carried by a Stealthed enemy** can't be targeted (Dispel, Shatter…), as the rulebook says. A targeted artifact must also be in its caster's region.
* **Disabled minions lose all their abilities**, just like silenced ones. Each of these kept working while disabled and now stops:
  * **Damage and death:** Seirawan Hydra no longer heals. Bruin, Doomsday Prophet, Goat for Azazel and Sir Gawain no longer change damage. Stitched Abomination no longer takes damage in parts. Sir Priamus no longer protects allies from lethal damage. Black Cat, Grim Reaper, Lady Iseult, The Doom of Dilmun and Young Master Damion no longer prevent or replace deaths, and The Doom of Dilmun can be banished.
  * **Untapping, mana and elements:** Rhitta Gawr of Snowdonia no longer stops other minions untapping. Älvalinne Dryads and Finwife give no extra mana. Granary Rats and Sinterfee no longer take away a site's elements. A site turned into a minion, such as Horns of Behemoth or Island Leviathan, gives no elements and no Scoria- or Tundra-style balancing.
  * **Movement and carrying:** Talamh Dreig and The Doom of Dilmun can be moved by force. Old Salt Anchorman no longer protects allies from being moved. Brobdingnag Bullfrog lets go of what it carries, and Sir Tom Thumb no longer climbs aboard. Megamoeba and Aethermoeba no longer occupy every square they pass.
  * **Combat:** Boudicca loses her bonus against sites. Sir Agravaine no longer triggers when an ally attacks. Sir Morien no longer blocks projectiles and White Hart no longer blocks attacks. Crawler and Crawling Congregation no longer let projectiles pass through. Yog-Sothoth no longer ignores sites. Order of the White Wing no longer intercepts units entering.
  * **Stealth, silence and magic:** Hounds of Ondaros, Hunting Party, Master Tracker, Scent Hounds and Sir Bors the Younger no longer strip Stealth. Sister Stefánia and Sisters of Silence no longer silence. Failed Mutation and Thing in the Ice are no longer protected from magic. Black Cat no longer controls random outcomes.
  * **Casting, summoning and granted abilities:** Caelestis no longer changes spell costs. Lucid Dreamers and Ominous Owl no longer let you summon on their square. Doomsday Cult, Grigori Rasputin, Salmon of Knowledge, Seasoned Sellsword, Skeleton and Vivien the Enchantress no longer give abilities to others. Shackled Demon still offers his release, since it's the way out of his disable.
  * **Identity:** Azuridge Caravan, Grösse Poltergeist, Monastery Gargoyle and Redmane Hyena lose the extra subtypes or elements they give themselves.
  * **Other cards:** a disabled King Arthur no longer returns stolen minions to their owners. A disabled minion entering the Saintweald doesn't fire its Deathrite as a Genesis.
* **Targeting** now follows one set of rules everywhere: the board highlights, spells cast by other cards, and both computer opponents offer exactly the targets the game accepts. For example, a second pick that depends on the first ("target minion nearby them") now highlights from the right unit, and the computer no longer tries targets the spell can't take. This fixes blink showing wrong destination squares when not cast from hand.
* **Tapping and untapping** now always go through the same rules:
  * "Doesn't untap the next time it would" (Sir Tristan, Waveshaper, Blasphemy) stops the next untap from any source, such as Lash or Sir Kay. Before, only the start-of-turn untap was stopped, and the effect could linger.
  * Rhitta Gawr's "Other minions can't untap" now also stops card effects, not just the start-of-turn untap.
  * Every card that taps a minion now counts for False Idol ("whenever a nearby minion taps"). Before, about thirty cards (Screamer, Mesmer Demon, Corpse Catapult's bearer…) tapped minions without it noticing.
* **Summoning sickness:** a minion that arrived this turn can't be tapped to pay a cost, as the rulebook says. This covers Corpse Catapult, Payload Trebuchet and Siege Ballista's bearer and helper, and Fae City's Faerie. Dhol Chants can still tap them: that's its effect, not a cost (FAQ).
* **Large minions and the void** follow the FAQ:
  * A large minion left over a square with no site is banished (Cloud City), and so is an animated aura minion. Before, a large minion slid somewhere else, and an aura minion went to the cemetery.
  * One straddling an edge that is no longer connected (Magellan Globe leaves, Flat Realm Society arrives) falls to the closest spot where it fits. You choose when two spots are equally close. With nowhere to fit, it dies.

#### Card fixes from the forced movement rework (geleeroyale) ####
* **13 Treasures of Britain**: slipping to a void puts the unit in the void, not on a surface.
* **Abaddon Succubus**: the 2 damage and the heal only happen if the minion actually arrives.
* **Aino**: crossing the edge is her own step, so an Immobile Aino stays, and she gains Stealth only if she crosses.
* **Angel's Egg / Devil's Egg** now work lying on the ground, the usual way to play them. Before, they only worked while carried.
* **Archangel Michael**: the step is mandatory, and choosing the square no longer breaks a warded site's Ward.
* **Bitter Departed**: only killers in its own region are offered, choosing one doesn't break a Ward, and it strikes only if it got there.
* **Call of the Sea**: units walk in on their own steps, then go under, unless something stops them (Talamh Dreig, Old Salt Anchorman, a submerge ward).
* **Cerberus in Chains** may step closer to its Avatar, never farther (FAQ). Before, it couldn't move at all.
* **Clamor of Harpies** strikes only a minion that arrived. Its Genesis is mandatory: if the cast skipped the target, it's asked for once the Harpies land.
* **Crave Golem / Persecutor**: "closest" counts their own steps, so prey across the void no longer wins. Persecutor walks instead of teleporting.
* **Darkest Dungeon** drags both units or neither (FAQ).
* **Displace**: a minion or artifact moves diagonally within its own region, rubble included, and a single-site aura may go to a void (FAQ). Choosing an enemy's warded site as the destination no longer breaks its Ward.
* **Draco Corvus** burrows only if it got there, only with a surface minion wholly on land that can be moved, and that minion's cargo goes under with it.
* **Fire Harpoons! / Lacuna Entity**: "target" now skips Stealthed enemies and breaks an enemy's Ward. Lacuna Entity only reaches a minion at an adjacent site.
* **Flanking Maneuver** asks which allies go when more than one is there.
* **Gargantula** cocoons only a minion that ends up at her location (FAQ). **Gloam Toads** may strike only a unit their drag brought in.
* **Grapple Shot, Pudge Butcher, Nelly Longarms, Wind Sylph, Riptide, Unland Eel, Megalurker**: these hits don't target, so a Ward no longer breaks and stops them.
* **Grasping Ground** also drags down an artifact lying above, and a dragged minion takes its gear along. It no longer offers a Stealthed enemy.
* **Into the Abyss**: you choose which adjacent void to pull into, and the unit goes under like any forced submerge (Cage of Sidrak, cargo, drowning).
* **Joust!** and **Innsmouth Dock** use real steps (walls, "enters here" effects), and an Old Salt Anchorman's ally no longer calls the joust off.
* **Kite Archer, Questing Beast, Bladedancer, Tringh Constrictor, Second Wind, Leap Attack** offer every legal step, Airborne diagonals included. A burrowed minion can come up with Leap Attack and strike the surface.
* **Led Astray** asks which enemies go and where, and each takes its own step. Before, every enemy moved and the destination prompt offered no squares.
* **Snowball** crosses the connected edge under Magellan Globe, like other projectiles.
* **Lure** only lets you pick an enemy minion nearby the chosen ally, so it can't fizzle after you pay.
* **Nelly Longarms** offers the dunk only once the unit has been dragged to her.
* **Old Salt Anchorman** doesn't stop an ally's own step, from Lure or Guile Sirens for example (FAQ).
* **Ormund Harpooneers** no longer offers a Stealthed enemy.
* **Pudge Butcher** may fight whatever his hook dragged in, an ally included.
* **Red Rock of Ravannis**: a bearer moves one step however many artifacts it carries; a bearer that can't be pulled loses the artifact instead (FAQ). Monuments stay.
* **Riptide** only pulls in units that aren't already on the site.
* **Rolling Boulder** keeps rolling under Magellan Globe until it would repeat a step (FAQ), stays in its region, and hits each unit once.
* **Sold-out Cemetery**: the site's controller picks the push direction.
* **Sturmgeist** now flies at the enemy that actually cast the Magic, such as a Spellcaster minion. Before, it always went after the enemy Avatar.
* **Thundering Giant** leaps within its own region and taps only the units where it lands, only if it lands.
* **Undertow** moves the unit by its own step and stays in the water. It no longer offers a Stealthed enemy, and its Genesis isn't optional.
* **Unland Eel** triggers only when it submerges, not when it's summoned or swims underwater.
* **Whirlwind / Windblast** never push Monuments. With Windblast, every unit moves at once, so a carried unit isn't blown two steps, and it wraps the edges under Magellan Globe.
* **Wills-o'-the-Wisp** may decline to evade, and stay in their region when they do.

#### Card fixes ####
* **Kairos the Archivist** puts auras back exactly where they were archived. Before, a 2x2 aura or a wall could come back on its old squares while still being treated as sitting where it had moved to since. A Thunderstorm that had drifted could then be animated by the Enchantress a step away from where it actually was.
* **Strikes from effects end Stealth:** a minion made to strike by a card, such as Whirling Blades, now loses its Stealth once the strikes are dealt, as it does when it attacks. An Infiltrated minion you spin with Whirling Blades still strikes your opponent's minions along its path, since it is yours while it moves, and then goes back to its owner.
* **Rift Valley** follows the FAQ again: it pulls apart two adjacent sites, one staying put while the other slides with everything beyond it. A lone site can't be pulled apart, and a site that can't be moved (Bedrock) stops the pull.
* **Intercepting** now ends a minion's Stealth, like attacking or defending does. Before, a Stealthed minion could intercept and stay hidden.
* **"Destroy all" effects** (Apply Alkahest, Boil, Unravel) destroy everything at once: every ward is checked first, so a Deathrite can't save the others.
* **Edge of the World** must always be next to the void: no site may be played into its last neighbouring void, and no Rift Valley, Earthquake or other shift may take it away.
* **Polar Bears, Polar Explorers and Ruler of Thul** can cross the top and bottom edges underground or underwater too, when they're burrowed or submerged.
* **Corpse Catapult** takes the only dead minion without asking, and its choice says which cemetery each comes from. It's no longer offered when the Bureau of Occult Control's toll can't be paid.
* **Thunderstorm** keeps track of where it has drifted. Before, an Enchantress animating a storm that had drifted put the creature back where the storm started, often over the void, where it died at once. An animated storm that drifts now moves its body too.
* **Fireball** hitting a large minion now centres on the square it actually hit.
* **Telekinesis** only offers an artifact nearby its caster. Before, you could pick one anywhere, and the spell fizzled after you paid. More generally, a "nearby" or "adjacent" artifact target is now checked for range like a unit's.
* **Rubble** that appears while The Flood is in play (from Geomancer, Star-seeds of Uhr, Old Mortimer's Den or the Lavamancer) is flooded, like the Rubble a destroyed site leaves.
* **Free City**: no more phantom "Free City" card beside the site after it defends. The city now leaves the fight as soon as the battle is over, and it no longer appears at all when you choose not to defend with it. If you decline, the city can still defend a later attack that turn.
* **Order of the White Wing** now banishes minions that weren't cast directly from a player's hand (FAQ). This includes cards held by Morgana le Fay, an Omphalos, Vivien or the Cradle of Etherrum, cards cast from your collection, and cards cast from a cemetery. Before, these got in.
* **Abyssal Assault** hits a unit in any region of a nearby square, such as one burrowed or submerged. Before, only the Monster's own region could be hit. All the damage is now dealt at once, as the FAQ says: a ward prevents every point, instead of breaking on the first and letting the rest through.
* **Blink** cast by an effect (Archimago's echo, a collection cast, a copy…) now offers the locations nearby the chosen ally. Before, it offered the locations nearby the caster.

#### Bot ####
* **The computer's Avatar stops roaming while it should be building.** Moving or attacking with the Avatar while it still has sites to play or draw now counts against it as a lost site play, with a weak Avatar marked down more. Before, this barely registered. Moving taps the Avatar, and the old check only looked at untapped Avatars. Also, the computer judges a turn only after playing out your reply, and early in the game several turns further, by which point it no longer saw what its Avatar did on the turn it was deciding.
* **Computer as the Frost Mage or Lavamancer** also avoids any Tap that isn't for a site before it has the elements its ability needs (Air and Water for the Frost Mage, Earth and Fire for the Lavamancer).
* **Computer site placement:** it no longer holds back from building outside its home area when that area is full, even if some of the sites there are yours. The same goes when its only empty home squares are ones it isn't allowed to play a site on.
