2026-11-08
#### A big thanks to waffles #### 
Waffles is developing their own unreleased game engine, and provided me a deep analysis of inconsistencies in untesed realm. Most of the fixes in this batch have been done after his findings. There are also some fixes done in response to your bug reports as usual

#### Rules fixes ####
* **Ranged shots are strikes.** A Ranged hit now works like any other strike:
  * a Lance adds +1 and breaks, and Blood Ravens heal you;
  * Phantasmal Shade is destroyed;
  * Interrogator and Men of Leng trigger on a hit Avatar;
  * Goswhit Helmet prevents it, Mask of Mayhem doubles it, Golden Harp caps it, Hemogolem heals you, and Doomsday Prophet no longer doubles it.
* **Ranged shots stop at a void square**, like spell projectiles already did, instead of flying over it.
* **Projectiles: the shooter chooses who is hit.** When a projectile can hit several units at one location, the shooter must pick one; a shot can no longer hit nobody.
* **Magic projectiles** (Magic Missiles and the like) now deal Magic damage, so Tragedy Worrywart stops them.
* **A fight is one damage event.** Every strike lands before anyone dies, including Lethal kills and Flaming Sword splashes, and every lethally damaged unit dies together. This fixes:
  * Squirming Mass growing mid-fight, and Mask of Mayhem losing its doubled strike back;
  * Fourberie Knot, Golden Harp and The Faerie Queene not seeing the whole fight;
  * Phantasmal Shade being destroyed before its own strike landed.
* **Kills in the same exchange still count.** A killer that dies in the same exchange of strikes still reacts to its kills: Blunderbore banishes, Grim Reaper banishes, and Sir Pellinore draws.
  * A minion waiting on its own Deathrite's question (Sacred Stag) counts as killed, so kill effects and The Great Famine / The Black Plague see the death.
* **Damage triggers wait for the whole effect.** "Whenever this takes damage" triggers (Flagellant) wait until all of that effect's damage has landed.
  * In a fight, the active player's damage triggers wait for the other side's deaths: Accursed Albatross no longer kills Army of the Dead's new Skeleton.
  * Sawbones heals only after the whole strike has landed.
* **Movers that die stop.** A minion that dies while moving (onto a Bottomless Pit, for example) no longer attacks or opens a Defend or intercept window.
* **Defenders and Hillock Basilisk.** A defender is no longer "at rest" once it starts walking, so it isn't disabled when it passes in front of Hillock Basilisk.
* **Being attacked or made to fight doesn't wake a minion.** A minion only stops being at rest when it enters, moves or is moved, casts a spell, or uses an ability (codex, At Rest). So a minion that Hillock Basilisk or Stone-gaze Gorgons has disabled stays disabled when it's attacked, or made to fight by Duel, Lord of Lies, Instigator Imp or Blaze of Glory, and doesn't strike. Fighters that moved or acted to get there (the attacker, defenders, Joust's riders, a minion dragged by Meat Hook or Pudge Butcher, Giant Shark, Awakened Mummies) still fight normally.
* **"When attacked" responses come first.** Responses that ask a question (Wills-o'-the-Wisp teleporting away) are answered before the fight; if the target got away, the attack ends.
* **"While attacking / defending" abilities** apply only in real attacks. In a plain fight (Giant Shark, an intercept), Escyllion Cyclops strikes back.
* **Carried artifacts act where their bearer is.** A carried Scarecrow keeps Airborne minions out, a carried Cold Iron Rod disables the Faerie holding it, and a carried Blightstone disables its bearer's site. Acid Rain over the bearer silences the carried artifact, so it gives its bearer nothing (+1 power, Ranged, Magnetic Muzzle's silence…).

* **Silencers follow timestamp order.** When silencers, disablers or sites switch each other off, the older one wins. That covers two Sister Stefánias, Stefánia against Hillock Basilisk or Stone-gaze Gorgons, and Fields of Phyxis against Smokestacks of Gnaak. A silencer that is itself silenced silences no one.
* **Plain moves settle the board at once.** A silence or Ward removal caused by a plain move (Sister Stefánia leaving, Order of the Pale Worm arriving, a Blightstone carrier) now applies immediately.
* **Illegal attack targets are refused when declared.** An attack declared on a unit that refuses it (Earl of the Ivory Towers, a Beast in Varmint Warrens, a Stealthed enemy) is now rejected outright. Before, the attacker moved and tapped first, then found it couldn't attack.
* **Targeting.**
  * Only texts that say "target" target. Wuthering Heights, Persecutor's brand and Doctor Demetrius no longer break Wards or skip Stealthed minions.
  * Bodach Bogeyman's pick-up does target.
  * Attacking a unit targets it.
  * Grievous Insult on a Warded minion now only spares that minion; the caster still draws.
* **Regions.**
  * "Nearby" effects stay in their own region: Accursed Albatross, Belfry, Order of the White Wing, Hounds of Ondaros and other Stealth-strippers.
  * Vesuvius and Blasphemy reach burrowed and submerged units.
  * Thundering Giant taps only units at its own level.
* **Forced submerge and burrow fail where they can't happen.** A forced submerge on land fails (Trial by Water no longer drowns land minions), and Rest in Peace only burrows on land. A push never changes a minion's region (River Rapids), and moving between a site's levels doesn't count as entering it (Old Mortimer's Den, Briar Patch).
* **Entry bans apply to summons.** Bans on entering a square ("can't enter", Crucifix) also stop tokens, effect summons and forced moves. Crucifix honours Saint of Redemption.
* **Can't be modified.** Cards that can't be modified (Monks of Kobalsa, The Doom of Dilmun) don't gain Stealth (Dark Alley, Infiltrate), artifact keywords or Flaming Sword's splash, and Infiltrate doesn't take control of them. The Doom of Dilmun can be moved by other effects.
* **Deathrites that leave play count as deaths.** A minion whose Deathrite takes it out of play (Sir Tom Thumb) still counts as dying. A carried "minion dies" relic (Alabaster Box, Bleeding Skull) sees its own bearer's death.
* **Floods.** Only Waveshaper taps minions when a flood fails on Bedrock. A site flooded by another card stays flooded while silenced (Smokestacks of Gnaak); only Mismanaged Mortuary's printed Flooded is lost, and it comes back when the silence ends.
* **Silence keeps Ward and Stealth.** A silenced minion or site keeps its Ward and Stealth marks, and its Ward still works. Only a disabled one loses them.
* **Carried minions and regions.** Being carried is forced movement, so a minion carried on top no longer follows its carrier when the carrier is forced into another region (Drown, Bury, Cave-In, a drag into the void). It stays where it is and stops being carried. The carrier's own burrow, submerge or void step still takes it along, and swallowed cargo (Brobdingnag Bullfrog) always goes along.
* **Lances.** A minion with "Lance, Lance, Lance" (Thankless Squire) enters with three Lances. A Lance minion summoned by another card (reanimated, conjured) now gets its Lances too.
* **Tap costs.** An artifact's "Tap →" ability can't be paid with a summoning-sick, disabled or tapped bearer (Holy Water, Meat Hook, Rhongomyniad, Silver Bullet, Spear of Destiny, Siege Ballista).
* **Magellan Globe** wraps Voidwalk and underground or underwater steps across its joined edges. Ruler of Thul, Polar Bears and Polar Explorers wrap the same way.

#### Card fixes ####
* **King Arthur** stops every steal of a minion at the moment it happens: Love Potion, Infiltrate, Puppet Master, Seasoned Sellsword and Peace Offering.
* **Vindictive Nation** charges for every site modified at once: a Ward on a site, Leadworks or Smokestacks silencing several sites, Acid Rain, and Mover of Mountains' swap.
* **The Manor at Daperyll Hill** heals you only when the full 3 damage is dealt, as the FAQ rules. If the damage is reduced, prevented or redirected, there is no heal. A warded target's Ward breaks instead, and Stealthed enemies can't be targeted. When the 3 damage kills, the victim's Deathrite resolves before you heal.
* **Jack the Ripper** can be cast anywhere a minion normally can, plus onto any Mortal. He could be cast onto any site before, even an empty enemy one.
* **The Ninth Legion** must still be cast to a corner you could normally summon to: your own corner site, or a void corner thanks to Voidwalk. It can no longer go onto an enemy's corner site.
* **Valor** and **Dodge Roll** can only be cast as responses (when an ally defends, or is attacked). They can no longer be cast in your main phase for nothing.
* **Joust!** and **Duel** only offer enemies adjacent to the ally you chose. A far-away enemy used to be accepted, and the spell then fizzled after you'd paid for it.
* **Gnarled Wendigo**: you now choose which of your minions on its square to sacrifice (any number, or none), and each one takes 2 off the cost. The sacrifice is paid with the mana, so they die even when the Wendigo enters disabled (for example, atop a burrowed Root Spider). Before, every minion there was taken, and a disabled Wendigo gave the discount without killing anyone.
* **Selfsame Simulacrum** copying a minion with a targeted Genesis now asks for that Genesis's targets. Before, the copied Genesis ran with no targets and did nothing.
* **Yog-Sothoth** occupies every location whenever a minion becomes Yog-Sothoth (Shapeshift and other transforms), even while silenced or disabled, and a Yog-Sothoth transformed into something else shrinks back. Its five-void check applies at once.
* **The Cities** (Glass, Plenty, Souls, Traitors): the whole "(X) — Provides (2) instead but …" clause, drawback included, applies only while you have that threshold.
* **Earl of the Ivory Towers** can't be targeted by anything Ordinary: spells, abilities or attacks.
* **Varmint Warrens** also stops enemies from attacking the Beasts there, since an attack targets the unit it's declared on.
* **Island Leviathan** and **Horns of Behemoth** awaken summoning sick if the site was played this turn (FAQ: only a site already in the realm before this turn awakens ready).
* **Craterize** always discards a site as its cost. With one site in hand it's discarded without asking; with several, the prompt can't be confirmed until one is picked. Before, confirming with nothing picked skipped the cost.
* **Balor of the Evil Eye**'s gaze isn't a projectile. It hits Stealthed enemies, isn't stopped by voids or projectile-blocking sites, and the direction preview shows the whole line.
* **Land Deed** takes control of its bearer's site at once (Rubble too), and the site goes back when no one bears the Deed there.
* **The Empyrean** always gives its 1 mana, plus one of each element while you control a nearby Angel or Warded unit or site.
* **Sir Lancelot** untaps after his first fight each turn (attacking, defending, intercepting or a forced fight), but not for a site strike or a Ranged shot hitting him.
* **Sir Kay** untaps after attacking a weaker minion. **Sir Gawain** takes damage for your Avatar too. **Sir Agravaine** strikes even when the attacking ally died in the fight.
* **Sir Pellinore** draws only for killing an enemy minion, compares split power by its average, and asks you on a tie for strongest.
* **Stygian Archers** make a Skeleton only for an enemy kill. A minion made to strike by someone else's effect (Backstab) uses its own strike and kill triggers.
* **Fourberie Knot** protects only a minion bearer, and not one that can't be modified.
* **Golden Harp** also caps damage to sites. 
* **Panpipes of Pnom** also raises ability damage from nearby units.
* **The Faerie Queene, Fourberie Knot, Sir Galahad and Sling Pixies** also block ability damage from qualifying units.
* **Hellstar** can intercept. **Hellstar and Lumbering Giant** can't defend, even on the attacked square.
* **Harassing Ruffians**: only their attacks on units can't be defended.
* **Lord of Destruction** breaks a Ward instead of killing the warded minion.
* **Lord of Fear**: with a single added defender, the attacked unit must stay in the fight.
* **Satanic Panic** no longer offers a Demon as its other target. **Sea of Ash** kills minions already below nearby sites when it is played.
* **Putrid Presence**: the subsurface of nearby sites can now really be attacked.
* **The Wild Hunt** gives Charge to every other nearby Spirit or Faerie, enemies included.
* **Wizard's Den** discards only when it is attacked successfully. **Sylvan Splendor** is dispelled by any successful attack on an affected site, even for 0, and draws only for real casts.
* **Wrath of the Sea** floods only sites next to water when it starts, no chain.
* **Begone!** can banish Failed Mutation.
* **Flaming Sword**'s splash from a Lethal bearer kills.
* **Rhongomyniad**'s throw hits one unit per location, enemies on the thrower's own square included.
* **Bodach Bogeyman**'s pick-up counts as entering the square (Troll Bridge strikes it). **Troll Bridge**'s strike is a real strike (Phantasmal Shade is destroyed).
* **Stealth** is lost after dealing damage with your own ability or a carried artifact (Bull Whip). **Balor** can pick his own Stealthed allies. **Siege Ballista** and **Tringh Constrictor** can't target Stealthed enemies.
* **Sneak Thief** can't target itself. **Ormund Harpooneers** can't tap with nothing to target.
* **Avalon** gives the opponent nothing while its abilities are gone.
* **Baba Yaga's Hut** can swap with Rubble. **Geomancer** can replace the Rubble he stands on.
* **Candlemas Monks** dying in the end phase no longer ends the opponent's next turn.
* **Chains of Prometheus** counts draws by an Omphalos, Morgana or Archangel Gabriel.
* **Wuthering Heights** and **Avatar of Air** don't offer squares the minion can't be moved into (Crucifix), units that ignore site abilities, or units that can't be moved by force.
* **Disintegrate** banishes the target's carried artifacts and units instead of deleting them.
* **Flame of the First Ones** still heals and drains when its bearer dies on arrival.
* **Giant Shark** judges its prey by where a unit entered.
* **Lucid Dreamers** give Voidwalk only to minions cast to their void.
* **Golgor** banishes all weak minions together.
* **Grigori Rasputin** pays his (2) and needs (F)(F) to return from the cemetery.
* **Harpyon Urge** strikes only if the ally arrives.
* **Innsmouth Dock** can lure subsurface minions into its subsurface.
* **Locusts of Illyria**'s copy can go on the Locusts' own square.
* **Mock Court** charges for a token that dies on arrival and for an end-of-turn token, and counts in Necromancer's Skeleton cost.
* **Gilded Aegis** saves a Phantasmal Shade only once.
* **Postern Gate** enters under the opponent's control even next to Smokestacks of Gnaak.
* **Realm-Eater** attacking a City of Souls still deals its strike.
* **Saintweald**'s echoed Nosferatu stays in play.
* **Scorched Earth** destroys carried artifacts and auras on the site too.
* **Seasoned Sellsword** needs a discard to be hired.
* **The Great Drowning of Men** and **Ablaze!** destroy the sites before the deaths, so Deathrites see Rubble.
* **The Green Knight**'s Deathrite strike is mandatory when there is a nearby enemy.
* **Thin Ice** doesn't submerge units that ignore site abilities.
* **Rolling Boulder** deals its own colourless damage.

#### Computer player ####
* The computer now considers casting Voidwalk minions into the void.

#### Interface ####
* Genesis targets can now be picked among underground, underwater and void units. For example, a **Lacuna Entity** in the void can now grab a burrowed **Root Spider** at an adjacent site. Clicking their small chips used to do nothing.
* The attack menu no longer offers attacks the rules forbid (Earl of the Ivory Towers, Scarecrow), and offers Putrid Presence's subsurface attacks.
