2026-08-07

#### New: Sealed mode (and collection fixes) ####
- You can now play Sealed (Limited)! Tick “🎴 sealed” when creating an online room to choose the pack edition (Alpha / Beta / Arthurian Legends / Gothic / Random), the number of packs, and a deck-construction timer. Both players open their own packs, build a deck against the clock (Spellbook ≥24, Atlas ≥12, no rarity limits), and click Ready — the game starts when both are ready or when the timer runs out (your deck is auto-filled from your pool). The Draft Kit supplies the Spellslinger avatar and unlimited basic Sites (Spire / Stream / Valley / Wasteland), and you keep every card you open. The play clock uses the ⏱ Clock setting.
- The public room list now shows each room’s game mode (📖 Constructed or 🎴 Sealed); joining a sealed room needs no pre-built deck.

- Opening a booster no longer dumps the cards into your collection before you’ve seen them — the cards join your collection as you reveal them (they still stay once revealed).
- Booster packs (both Sealed pools and the Collection minigame) now contain Avatars of their edition, at rare rarity — just like real packs.
- Your collection now follows your account: signing in shows that account’s collection, signing out clears it from the browser, and switching accounts no longer leaves the previous account’s cards behind (a guest’s cards still migrate up on first sign-in).

#### UI fixes ####
- On desktop the whole game now scales to fit your screen so the view is identical on any monitor — the same layout on a small laptop, a 1080p screen, or a 4K display, just larger or smaller (no more tiny board on big screens or clipped panels on small ones).
- Activating a site’s ability is easier to click: the whole site is now the hitbox (any part not covered by a minion or another card), instead of a thin janky strip.
- When the server is about to restart, a countdown banner now appears at the top-centre of every page (“Server restarts in Nm Ss — all ongoing battles will end”), dismissable with an ✕, so games in progress get fair warning.

#### General ruling fixes ####
- Disabled minions no longer sneak their start/end-of-turn triggers past the disable. For example, a Locusts of Illyria that has been disabled (Freeze, Monstermorphosis chrysalis, etc.) no longer spreads a copy at end of turn.

#### Card fixes ####
- Maelström: its owner now pulls in every minion in the body of water — their own and the opponent's — and chooses the pull direction when two squares both draw a minion closer. Your opponent is never prompted about their own minions.
- Monstermorphosis cast on an enemy minion now leaves the resulting Monster (from your hand) or Horrible Hybrids (from your collection) under your opponent's control — a transform never changes who controls a minion. It is still your card, so it goes to your cemetery when it dies.
