#### Rules fixed ####
* The **route picker** no longer hides routes that pass over the destination and come back to it.
* **Monuments** can never be carried: they can't be handed to a unit when cast, and **Telekinesis** and **Lord of Greed** can't take them. Previously they were just excluded from pickup
* A **silenced** site that grants extra steps (e.g. **Astral Alcazar**) no longer grants them.

#### Card fixes ####
* **Town Priest** can target itself when it's Evil, so your favourite combo will work here too
* **Orb of Ba'al Berith** was a complete bugged mess, fixed
* **Deathspeaker** now checks thresholds
* **Tournament Grounds** now works with **Vanguard Knights** properly (it checked for threshold before only for them)
* **Black Mass** and **Browse** drawn cards are now hidden. You can also properly order card you put at the bottom
* **Skeleton Mage**: the magic it casts from a cemetery is banished afterwards and no longer comes back into a cemetery.
* **Awakened Mummies** can't surface on their own anymore
* **Trial by Water**, **Harpyon Urge**, **Consecrate**/**Desecrate**, **Willing Tribute** and **Dream-Quest** now can select your avatar as an ally (dream quest would do nothing but you can still cast it)
* **Just a Rock** can be cast onto an enemy that is carrying an artifact.

#### More cards and rules fixes — thanks to aadu-gm (playsorceryonline.com), and geleeroyale (realms.cards) ###
* **Geomancer** counts a **Bloom** site (Desert, Twilight, Algae) as an earth site the turn it comes down. (aadu-gm)
*  one **Pick Up** can take artifacts and units together (a War Horse grabs its Lance *and* its rider) (geleeroyale)
* **Mariner's Curse** no longer triggers when a minion just surfaces or submerges in place (geleeroyale)
* **Giant Shark** surfaces to fight prey on its own site instead of fighting it from underwater (geleeroyale)
* a projectile cast by another effect now shows the aiming arrows on the board (geleeroyale)

#### BOT ####
* The **Thinking** computer is about **three times faster** and slightly stronger
* the computer can **Blink** an ally that isn't next to its caster. (geleeroyale)
