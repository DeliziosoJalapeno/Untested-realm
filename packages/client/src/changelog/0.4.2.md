2026-10-05

#### New Coordinates ####

* **Switched to 1-20 numbering for sites** In ⚙ Settings, pick your coordinate system: **Standard** (squares numbered 1–20, the new default), **Chess** (a1–e4), or **Cartesian** ((1,1)–(5,4)). Whatever you pick shows **everywhere** — the board overlay, the game log, and prompts such as a movement path.

#### UI improvements and fix ####

* new homemade paint art (waiting for the commissioned one...)
* **Online replays.** Now they actually work
* **Settings moved to the home screen.** The ⚙ Settings button left the (already packed) in-game board — set sound, pop-ups and coordinates before you play.
* **GitHub & Discord.** Links to the project's source and community now sit under the title on the home page.
* **New quick-chat phrases:** ":(", "sorry".
* **Tidier default decks.** The bundled community decks now have English names, and near-duplicates were pruned so the list is shorter and clearer.

#### Card Fixes ####

* **Genesis finishes before bottomless acts.** A minion summoned straight onto Bottomless Pit (or a relocating site like Dark Alley) now resolves its Genesis in full — **including choosing its targets** — before the site kills or moves it.
* **Abyssal Assault won't offer a dead target.** When an earlier projectile had already killed a unit, it could still appear as a choice for the next one; it's now excluded.
