2026-10-09

#### Rules & card fixes ####
* **Sister Stefánia** now only silences other minions in **her own region** — a minion burrowed or submerged beneath her (or she beneath it) is no longer silenced.
* **Archimago** — you may now choose **which of your spellcasters** casts the echoed spell (per the FAQ), so a projectile copy can be fired from a well-placed minion instead of always from your avatar. (Targetless copies still auto-cast, no needless prompt.)
* **Arcane Barrage** cast through **Merlin's Tower** now gets the tower's "costs ③ less" applied to its **X** — so X ≤ 3 is **free** (and you can fire up to three projectiles with no mana to spend).
* **Ranged strikes are strikes** — a **Grim Guisarme** now doubles a ranged strike's damage, both dealt (bearer shooting) and taken (bearer shot), the same as a melee strike.
* **Skirmishers of Mu** — their mid-march volley is now a **real ranged strike** (it counts for Grim Guisarme, fires on-kill/after-strike triggers, respects Lethal and cover). You pick **which square along your march** to shoot from — the starting square included — and it only happens if the Skirmishers actually reach that square.
* **Kite Archer** — its after-a-ranged-strike step is now **optional** (a "stay put" choice is offered) and it **slides** to the new square instead of playing a teleport "zap."
