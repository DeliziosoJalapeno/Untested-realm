2026-10-09

#### Online & rooms ####
* **You can't join your own room as both players** anymore — a second tab or device on the same account (or the same connection) is blocked from taking the empty seat, and matchmaking will never pair you against yourself.
* **Close your rooms** — a "Close room" button on the waiting screen deletes a room you created, and the Home "Your games" list has a ✕ to close any of your rooms from anywhere. (Plain "Leave" still keeps the room open for rejoin.)
* **Create a room *and* stay in the queue** — "Find a battle" now runs independently, so you can open a room to invite a friend while still matchmaking. Whichever starts first wins: a pairing closes your open rooms, and a room starting takes you out of the queue.

#### Rules & card fixes ####
* **The Round Table** now draws for every Knight, Sir, or Dame — including **"Knight"-named minions** it used to ignore (Black / Blue / Yellow / White / Death / Brother Knight, **Vanguard Knights**, The Green Knight) as well as King Arthur. (**Templar**, **Tournament Grounds** and **Knighthood** now recognise the same set, including the plural "Vanguard Knights".)
* **The Void** ("this site is also void") now **banishes** any minion without Voidwalk placed on it — surface or subsurface — instead of letting it sit there safely. Voidwalk minions still live there.
* **Sister Stefánia** now only silences other minions in **her own region** — a minion burrowed or submerged beneath her (or she beneath it) is no longer silenced.
* **Archimago** — you may now choose **which of your spellcasters** casts the echoed spell (per the FAQ), so a projectile copy can be fired from a well-placed minion instead of always from your avatar. (Targetless copies still auto-cast, no needless prompt.)
* **Arcane Barrage** cast through **Merlin's Tower** now gets the tower's "costs ③ less" applied to its **X** — so X ≤ 3 is **free** (and you can fire up to three projectiles with no mana to spend).
* **Ranged strikes are strikes** — a **Grim Guisarme** now doubles a ranged strike's damage, both dealt (bearer shooting) and taken (bearer shot), the same as a melee strike.
* **Skirmishers of Mu** — their mid-march volley is now a **real ranged strike** (it counts for Grim Guisarme, fires on-kill/after-strike triggers, respects Lethal and cover). You pick **which square along your march** to shoot from — the starting square included — and it only happens if the Skirmishers actually reach that square.
* **Kite Archer** — its after-a-ranged-strike step is now **optional** (a "stay put" choice is offered) and it **slides** to the new square instead of playing a teleport "zap."

#### Deck builder ####
* The deck **Import** panel now notes that only **public** decks can be imported — set the deck's visibility to public on its source site first.
