2026-10-08

#### GUI fixes ####
* **Target-a-water-site / target-a-land-site spells** now glow their legal locations the same bright way a minion summon or Minor Explosion does — **Stormy Seas**, **Riptide**, **Baptize**, **Call of the Sea**, **Dredge**, **Exhume**, and **Cave-In** all show you exactly where you can aim (engine-decided, no guessing).
* **Two-by-two auras** (and **Earthquake**'s rearrange) can now be placed overhanging a corner or edge — but only when a **Magellan Globe** (or similar edge-wrapping effect) is in play, matching how the board actually wraps. Without one, the overhanging anchors are hidden and rejected.
* A site's **🤐 silenced badge** shifts a little to the right when a two-by-two aura sits on that square, so the two icons no longer overlap.
* **Questing Beast** — its end-of-turn step now slides across the board like a normal move instead of playing a teleport "zap." (It was also skipping on-enter triggers like Briar Patch and Dark Alley; those now fire for the step.)

#### Rules & card fixes ####
* **Silenced artifacts now do nothing** except be carried around. Previously a silenced artifact kept running all of its passive effects — most visibly a silenced **Magellan Globe** still wrapped the board's edges. Now silence switches off every passive artifact ability. Affected cards include **Magellan Globe** (edge-wrap + movement), the four **Cores** and **Shrine of the Dragonlord** (threshold), **Iron Shackles**, **Cage of Sidrak**, **Cold Iron Rod** (disabling), **The Rack**, **Grim Guisarme**, **Screaming Skull**, **Tabula Rasa**, **Blade of Thorns**, **Magnetic Muzzle**, **Panpipes of Pnom**, **Driftwood Marrows** (worn gear), **13 Treasures of Britain**, **Red Rock of Ravannis** (pick-up / carry), **Book of the Dead**, **Gilded Aegis** (death replacement), **Blasted Oak**, **Mask of Mayhem** (compulsion), **Bailey**, **Crucifix**, **Scarecrow** (entry bans), **Battering Ram**, **Rolling Boulder**, **The Pallid Bust** (granted abilities), and every tick-each-turn device — **Doomsday Device**, **The Immortal Throne**, **Angel's Egg**, **Devil's Egg**, **Nadir Seed**, **Pendulum of Peril**, **Sentinel Trap**, **Land Deed**, **Black Obelisk**, **Crave Golem**, **Tombstone Wardens**, **Torshammar Trinket**. (A silenced **Automaton** still moves, attacks, blocks, and taps for costs like any silenced minion.)
* **Atlantean Fate** no longer silences **Ordinary** sites — rubble and other ordinary locations keep working under it; only non-Ordinary sites are silenced.
