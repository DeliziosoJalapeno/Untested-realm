#### Deck builder ####

* **Filter cards by mechanic.** A new **🔧 Filters** panel narrows the card pool by what cards actually *do* — removal, card draw, ramp, summon, buffs, kill / destroy / banish, triggers (Genesis, Deathrite, tap abilities), keywords (Airborne, Ward, Stealth, Lethal, Ranged…), subtypes (Undead, Dragon, Beast…) and more. Combine freely: choices in the same row are OR'd, different rows are AND'd — so you can ask for e.g. *(Kill or Banish) and Airborne*.
* **Filter by mana cost and thresholds.** Beside the mechanic chips is a slider widget: set a **mana-cost** range and per-element **threshold** minimums *and* maximums (Air / Earth / Fire / Water) — e.g. find every card that costs 2–4 and needs exactly two Fire.
* **Filter by rarity.** A rarity dropdown (Ordinary / Exceptional / Elite / Unique) now sits alongside the type and element filters.
* **Live deck analysis.** When you're not hovering a card, the right column shows a read-out of the deck you're building: mechanic coverage (how much removal / draw / ramp / etc. it runs), a mana curve, and its element balance. Purely descriptive — no nagging, since Sorcery decks legitimately skip whole categories.
* **Standard element colours.** Elements in the deck builder now use the exact same Air / Earth / Fire / Water colours as the in-game board.

#### Movement ####

* **Move out and back to your own site.** A unit with movement to spare (2+ steps) can step out to an adjacent location and return, ending where it started — its own site is now offered as a move destination, marked with a ↻ badge. Handy when you want to *move* without changing position (to re-trigger "when a unit enters" effects, and the like). If several bounce routes exist, you choose which way it goes.
* **Manual movement no longer force-stops at your destination.** Routing a move by hand and reaching your target square with movement still left now offers a **⏹ Stop here / ▶ Keep moving** choice, instead of ending the move automatically — so you can press on or loop back.

#### Fixes ####

* **Second Seer works now — and at the right time.** The optional rule was broken: turning it on and trying to use the peek did nothing (it was wrongly tied to whichever avatar you fielded, so the prompt had no valid follow-up). It's been rebuilt as a self-contained effect and now happens **once at the very start of the game — right after mulligans, before turn 1** — rather than on the second player's first turn.
* **Sold-out Cemetery no longer pushes the same Undead twice.** When more than one Undead entered the site in a single resolution (a move that loops back through it, or a Skeleton dropped onto it), "push another Undead away" could shove — and ask the direction for — the *same* resident twice. Each Undead is now pushed exactly once; every distinct entry still evicts, as intended.
* **2×2 aura cards sit on their intersection again.** A 2×2 area aura's card image is centred on the shared corner of its four sites; it only shifts aside when an oversized 2×2 creature stands on the same block and would otherwise cover it.
